Family grammar

Consistency comes from construction, not identical bodies.

  • Faceless posture and action carry expression
  • Heavy 3-unit navy construction lines
  • Cream and pale-blue working bodies
  • Warm color marks the active decision or object
  • Course action changes the silhouette
  • Small props remain structural, never decorative
