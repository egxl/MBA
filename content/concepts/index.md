## 🌐 The Cross-Curricular Knowledge Lattice

The **P3MD MBA Knowledge Matrix** compiles and synthesizes core business mechanisms across all six foundational courses (**MK 1 – MK 6**) and the **Rabuan Executive Series**.

Unlike conventional academic wikis that silo knowledge by course, the Knowledge Matrix establishes an **interconnected semantic lattice**: when a concept is analyzed in multiple disciplines (such as _Resource Allocation_ across Organizational Behavior, Finance, and Operations), it is synthesized into a single compounding reference.

## 🏛️ Six Core Business Pillars

<div class="knowledge-pillars-grid">

### 1. Strategy & Enterprise Governance (MK 1 & MK 5)

Foundational frameworks governing organizational direction, competitive advantage, and executive alignment:

- **[[concepts/ikigai|Ikigai Framework]]**: Convergence of passion, vocation, mission, and profession.
- **[[concepts/leadership-ambidexterity|Leadership Ambidexterity]]**: Balancing operational exploitation (core efficiency) with strategic exploration (new revenue engines).
- **[[concepts/self-awareness|The Self-Awareness Triad]]**: Internal vs. external mirrors, eliminating perceptual blind spots.
- **[[concepts/values-congruence|Values Congruence & Executive Friction]]**: Alignment between authentic personal values and daily resource allocation.
- **[[concepts/identity-based-living|Identity-Based Living & Strategy]]**: Grounding execution in _"Who do I want to become?"_ rather than glorified to-do lists.
- **[[concepts/wheel-of-life|The Wheel of Life Diagnostic]]**: 8-wedge personal resource and satisfaction spider diagram.
- **[[concepts/purpose-to-impact|Purpose-to-Impact Planning]]**: Backward-chaining purpose into 3–5 year and 30-day impact milestones.

### 2. Financial Management & Capital Strategy (MK 2)

Analytical mechanisms governing valuation, capital budgeting, and shareholder value creation:

- **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: Time-value valuation and enterprise free cash flow projection. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)**: The blended hurdle rate for enterprise investment decisions. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Capital Rationing**: Prioritizing positive-NPV projects under finite balance-sheet constraints. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_

### 3. Marketing & Customer Value Creation (MK 3)

Market sensing, customer lifetime value, and competitive positioning:

- **Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)**: Understanding customer circumstance and functional/emotional hiring drivers. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: The net present value of long-term customer relationships. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Segmentation, Targeting & Positioning (STP)**: Strategic market partition and value proposition architecture. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_

### 4. Operations & Supply Chain Flow (MK 4)

Process throughput, network coordination, and bottleneck optimization:

- **[[concepts/service-blueprinting|Service Blueprinting]]**: 5 horizontal layers, 3 boundaries, and isolating causal root failure from experienced customer pain.
- **[[concepts/customer-order-decoupling-point|Customer Order Decoupling Point (CODP)]]**: Strategic inventory and informational buffering separating forecast from customer demand (MTS, ATO, MTO, ETO).
- **[[concepts/service-system-design-matrix|Service-System Design Matrix]]**: Balancing customer contact, process efficiency, customization opportunity, and worker diagnostic skill profiles.
- **[[concepts/customer-introduced-variability|Customer-Introduced Variability]]**: Diagnosing 5 types of customer variability and applying uncompromised reduction strategies.
- **[[concepts/littles-law|Little's Law]]**: Fundamental queueing theorem coupling Work-in-Process ($WIP$), throughput rate ($\lambda$), and flow time ($W$).
- **Theory of Constraints (TOC)**: Maximizing enterprise throughput by elevating the primary constraint. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **The Bullwhip Effect**: Amplification of demand variance across upstream supply tiers. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_

### 5. Problem Solving, Decision Making & Negotiation (MK 5)

Cognitive clarity, game theory, and strategic agreement design:

- **BATNA**: Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement and reservation price determination. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Zone of Possible Agreement (ZOPA)**: The bargaining overlap enabling mutually advantageous deals. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
- **Cognitive Biases in Leadership**: Overconfidence, sunk cost fallacy, and framing distortions. _(Ingestion Scheduled)_

### 6. Applied Business Analytics & Human Capital (MK 6 & Rabuan)

Quantitative decision science, predictive modeling, and people analytics:

- **[[concepts/talent-density|Talent Density Power Law]]**: Pareto distributions in high-cognition creative enterprise output.
- **[[concepts/compa-ratio|Compa-Ratio Banding]]**: Auditing internal pay equity against market midpoints.
- **Turnover Drag Multiplier**: Modeling fully-loaded voluntary attrition friction ($1.5\times-2.5\times$ salary). _(Ingestion Scheduled)_
