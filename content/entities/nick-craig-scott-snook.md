## 👥 Profile & Academic Collaboration

**Nick Craig** (President of the Authentic Leadership Institute) and **Scott Snook** (Senior Lecturer of Business Administration, Harvard Business School MBA Class of 1958) are executive educators specializing in authentic leadership development, executive purpose articulation, and high-impact organizational transformation.

---

## 🧠 Core Frameworks & Contributions

### 1. From Purpose to Impact (HBR 2014)

Craig and Snook pioneered the **Purpose-to-Impact** methodology, training over 1,000 corporate executives across Fortune 500 multinationals (including Unilever, GE, and Heineken):

- **The Core Paradox**: Fewer than 20% of global leaders possess an articulated individual purpose statement.
- **The Backward-Chaining Blueprint**: Translating declarative personal purpose into 3–5 year aspirations, 2-year realities, 1-year narrative shifts, and 30-day small wins.
- **The Shore Team**: Emphasizing that individuals cannot discover their authentic purpose in isolation—they require trusted truth-tellers to act as reflective mirrors.
