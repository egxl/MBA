## 👤 Executive & Leader Profile

**Jenderal TNI (Kehormatan) (Purn.) Djamari Chaniago** is a retired senior Indonesian Army (TNI AD) flag officer who served as **Menteri Koordinator Bidang Politik dan Keamanan Republik Indonesia (2025–2026)** and previously held board oversight appointments as Commissioner and President Commissioner in Indonesian state-owned enterprises (BUMN).

On Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at **77 years of age** and after receiving the speaking invitation only the previous evening, he declined to postpone the session and delivered an afternoon executive address to the **P3MD Batch I Soedirman** cohort at Kampus DCU Nawasena without slides or visual aids—titled **"Pengabdian Tanpa Batas: Kepemimpinan, Disiplin, dan Kesiapan Menjawab Panggilan Negara"**. His physical poise and unscripted delivery served as a living demonstration of the soldier's creed that national service knows no retirement.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

Jenderal Djamari Chaniago distilled decades of military command and BUMN boardroom oversight into five rigorous operational principles:

1. **Discipline as Breath & Three-Stage Habit Formation**:
   - Anchors command culture in the maxim received since his days as an Army captain: _"Disiplin itu napasmu; kesetiaan kepada bangsa adalah kebanggaan; kehormatan adalah segala-galanya."_
   - Explains that discipline matures through three stages: **Paksaan** (external enforcement) $\rightarrow$ **Kebiasaan** (habitual repetition) $\rightarrow$ **Kesadaran** (internalized conscience), reinforced by his operational buffer-time rule: **"Tepat waktu itu sudah terlambat."**
2. **Meritocracy by Performance Alone**:
   - Insists that rules override personal affinity (_"yang melarang saya adalah ketentuan"_): leaders must never promote favored subordinates who fail qualification standards nor block qualified personnel due to personal dislike.
   - Instructs emerging BUMN officers that the sole legitimate channel to superiors is **work achievement (_prestasi_)**—rejecting personal gifts (_oleh-oleh_), domestic flattery, or lobbying for positions (_pantang meminta jabatan_).
3. **Uncompromising Conscience in BUMN Governance**:
   - Shares his personal decision to resign prematurely from BUMN Commissioner/President Commissioner posts when conscience could not accept prevailing irregularities and his leverage was insufficient to reform them, reminding the cohort: _"Kebohongan berjalan lebih cepat, tetapi kejujuran pasti akan sampai."_
4. **Confronting the Resource Governance Paradox**:
   - Contrasts Indonesia's natural wealth against systemic leakage—highlighting capture fisheries potential of roughly **USD 35 billion/year** undermined by **~16,000 illegal foreign vessels** (2024), coal export proceeds retained offshore, and corrupt proceeds hoarded _"di bawah bantal"_—concluding that institutional failure is fundamentally human: _"Kalau mau ada seribu cara, kalau tidak mau ada sepuluh ribu alasan."_
5. **Gradual Institutional Reform from Within (_Jangan Ragu, Tetapi Jangan Sekarang_)**:
   - Advises junior BUMN entrants not to fight blindly before building operational footing: first establish competence, procedural mastery, and a credible track record, mature decisions collaboratively with staff (never from an ivory tower / _menara gading_), and escalate principled corrections with data and solutions through formal channels.
