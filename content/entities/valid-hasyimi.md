## 👤 Academic & Professional Profile

**Dr. Valid Hasyimi** is an Assistant Professor and Lead Faculty member at the **School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung (SBM ITB)**. He serves as the primary course coordinator and lead lecturer for **MK 5: Problem Solving, Decision Making and Negotiation** within the P3MD (_Pendidikan Profesional Berkelanjutan_) Executive MBA program.

His academic research and executive coaching focus on:

- **Behavioral Decision Making**: System 1 vs. System 2 cognitive processing, heuristic traps, and managerial biases under extreme volatility.
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA)**: Mathematical application of SMART and AHP in capital allocation and public sector strategy.
- **Rational Problem Solving**: Institutional deployment of Kepner-Tregoe (KT) methodologies across Indonesian state-owned enterprises (BUMN).
- **Strategic Negotiation**: Integrative deal structuring, multiparty coalition dynamics, and principled bargaining in corporate governance.

## 📚 Core Pedagogical Literature & Frameworks

Dr. Valid Hasyimi grounds executive decision analysis in authoritative international decision science literature:

- Max H. Bazerman & Don A. Moore: _Judgment in Managerial Decision Making_ (Wiley).
- Paul Goodwin & George Wright: _Decision Analysis for Management Judgment_ (Wiley).
- Charles H. Kepner & Benjamin B. Tregoe: _The New Rational Manager_ (Princeton Press).
- Roger Fisher, William Ury, & Bruce Patton: _Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In_ (Penguin).
- Leigh Thompson: _The Mind and Heart of the Negotiator_ (Pearson).
