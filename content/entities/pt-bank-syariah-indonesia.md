## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Bank Syariah Indonesia Tbk** (IDX: `BRIS`) is the premier Islamic commercial banking institution in Indonesia and a cornerstone of the national Islamic financial ecosystem. Officially established on February 1, 2021 (19 Jumadil Akhir 1442 H), BSI represents the landmark strategic merger of three state-owned Islamic banking entities: **PT Bank Syariah Mandiri**, **PT BNI Syariah**, and **PT Bank BRIsyariah Tbk**.

Headquartered in Jakarta, BSI operates under majority ownership by state banking champions (**PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk**, **PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (Persero) Tbk**, and **PT Bank Negara Indonesia (Persero) Tbk**) and is positioned within the sovereign **[[entities/danantara|Danantara]]** asset architecture. With total assets exceeding Rp 350 trillion, BSI ranks among the top 10 largest commercial banks in Indonesia and is actively targeting a top-10 global Islamic banking footprint.

BSI operates across consumer banking, micro-retail, SME financing, wholesale corporate banking, and **Treasury & Global Markets**. Its operations are strictly anchored in Sharia compliance overseen by the Dewan Pengawas Syariah (DPS) and the National Sharia Board of MUI (DSN-MUI), utilizing contracts including _Murabahah_ (cost-plus markup financing), _Mudharabah_ and _Musyarakah_ (profit-loss sharing partnerships), _Ijarah_ (leasing and usufruct contracts), and _Wakalah_ (agency mandates).

### Business Immersion 1 (October 2026): Treasury, Liquidity & Halal Value Chain

- **Core Operational Dilemma**: Managing structural treasury liquidity and foreign exchange hedging under Sharia constraints where conventional interest rate swaps and speculative derivatives are impermissible, while funding high-capital BUMN infrastructure projects.
- **The Islamic Banking Engine**:
  - _Treasury & Global Markets Operations_: Developing compliant liquidity absorption instruments, including Sukuk sovereign debt securities, interbank Sharia money market tools (PUAS), and foreign exchange forward contracts based on _Wa'ad_ (unilateral binding promise).
  - _Halal Ecosystem Anchor_: Channeling wholesale financing into certified halal consumer goods, Islamic pilgrimage (Hajj and Umrah) travel escrow accounts, Muslim fashion, and Islamic education campuses.
  - _Digital Onboarding & Youth Penetration_: Deploying mobile banking platforms (BYOND by BSI) to attract millennial and Gen-Z retail depositors through ethical investment and automated Zakat/Waqf philanthropy features.
- **Strategic Alignment with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**:
  - Advancing national financial literacy and Sharia market share (historically lagging below 8% in the world's most populous Muslim nation) while delivering competitive return on equity (ROE > 16%) comparable to top-tier conventional peers.
