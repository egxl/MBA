## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Aviasi Pariwisata Indonesia (Persero)**, operating under the master brand **InJourney**, is Indonesia's state-owned holding conglomerate dedicated to aviation, hospitality, retail, and tourism destination ecosystems. Established by Government Regulation No. 104/2021 as part of the Ministry of BUMN's cluster rationalization program, InJourney integrates state assets across the complete traveler journey.

Operating within the sovereign development framework of **[[entities/danantara|Danantara]]**, InJourney coordinates six primary operating sub-clusters:

1. **InJourney Airports**: Unifying **PT Angkasa Pura I** and **PT Angkasa Pura II** into a unified airport management giant overseeing 35+ commercial airports across Indonesia (including Soekarno-Hatta International Airport `CGK`, I Gusti Ngurah Rai `DPS`, and Kualanamu `KNO`).
2. **InJourney Retail (PT Sarinah)**: Indonesia's first modern department store and national showcase for domestic MSMEs, Indonesian crafts, and cultural heritage products.
3. **InJourney Destination**: Destination management authority for national cultural landmarks including Candi Borobudur, Prambanan, and Ratu Boko, as well as the Mandalika International Circuit and Labuan Bajo waterfront.
4. **InJourney Hospitality (Hotel Indonesia Group)**: Managing state-owned hotel chains, historic heritage properties (Hotel Indonesia Kempinski), and convention centers.
5. **InJourney Aviation Services (IAS)**: Providing ground handling, airside cargo logistics, catering, and airport movement services.

InJourney operates to transform Indonesia's fragmented tourism and aviation sectors into a world-class engine capable of boosting international visitor spend, reducing passenger connecting friction, and lifting tourism's contribution to national GDP.

### Business Immersion 1 (October 2026): Airport Operations & Sarinah Cultural Ecosystem

- **Core Operational Dilemma**: Managing extreme flight punctuality and baggage turnaround bottlenecks across high-traffic airside facilities, while pivoting airport financial models from aeronautical fees toward high-margin non-aeronautical retail, culinary, and digital passenger experiences.
- **The InJourney Integration Blueprint**:
  - _Airside & Airport Movement Logistics_: Streamlining ground handling, security checkpoints, and runway slot allocation across InJourney Airports to minimize turnaround delays and passenger dwell frustration.
  - _Sarinah Curatorial Commerce_: Elevating local regional micro-enterprises into high-end retail brands through standardized packaging, export certification, and premium urban retail display.
  - _Destination Synergy_: Cross-selling air travel, domestic airport transfers, hotel accommodations, and heritage site ticketing under a unified digital loyalty and booking architecture.
- **Strategic Alignment with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**:
  - Maintaining remote pioneer airstrips and national cultural heritage monuments (requiring heavy conservation and structural archeological investments) while maximizing commercial EBITDA margins across prime international gateways.
