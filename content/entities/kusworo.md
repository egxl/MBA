## 👤 Executive & Biographical Genesis

**Marsekal Madya TNI (Purn) Kusworo, S.E., M.M.** is a retired three-star Flag Officer of the Indonesian Air Force (TNI AU), veteran flight instructor, former **Komandan Sesko TNI**, and former **Kepala Badan Nasional Pencarian dan Pertolongan (Kabasarnas)**. Combining decades of military aviation command, strategic joint-service education leadership, and high-stakes national disaster rescue operations, Marsdya Kusworo embodies the archetype of the operational crisis commander whose leadership doctrine is forged in life-and-death operational environments.

Born in Gombong, Central Java, on February 12, 1967, Marsdya Kusworo entered military aviation through the Indonesian Air Force Academy (_Akademi Angkatan Udara_ / AAU), graduating with the Class of 1988. He completed the TNI AU Flying School (_Sekolah Penerbang TNI AU_ / Sekbang) in 1990 and dedicated much of his operational aviation career to the demanding field of flight instruction and operational training. He earned qualifications from the General Instructor Course (1994), Flight Instructor Course (1994), and graduated from the prestigious TNI AU Flight Instructor School (_Sekolah Instruktur Penerbang_ / SIP) in 1996.

His advanced joint and national defense education includes Sekkau A-62 (1997), Seskoau A-39 (2003), Sesko TNI A-38 (2011), and the highest national strategic education at Lemhannas RI through the Program Pendidikan Reguler Angkatan (PPRA) LIII in 2015. Alongside his military schooling, he earned bachelor's and master's degrees in management (S.E., M.M.).

## 🎖️ Career Appointments & Command Chronology

Marsdya Kusworo's military and national service record spans primary flight instruction, operational air base command, strategic personnel management, tri-service staff college leadership, and national disaster response command:

- **Instruktur Penerbang Lanud Adi Sutjipto**: Formative instruction of young cadet pilots in flight fundamentals, aerobatics, and formation flight.
- **Komandan Lanud Adi Soemarmo (Solo, 2011–2013)**: Command of a major military aviation training base responsible for basic officer and specialist airman development.
- **Paban VI/Binprof Sopsau (2014)**: Supervision of aircrew professional development and operational standards at Air Force Headquarters.
- **Paban I/Renstra Srenau (2016)**: Long-range strategic capability planning and defense budgetary programming for TNI AU.
- **Kadisdikau (2016–2018)**: Oversight of all educational institutions, flight training curricula, and academic programs across the Air Force.
- **Wadan Seskoau (2018–2019)**: Joint academic and doctrine leadership at the Air Force Command and Staff College in Lembang.
- **Kadisopslatau (2019–2020)**: Operational readiness, air exercises, joint doctrine execution, and flight safety enforcement across TNI AU.
- **Aspotdirga Kasau (2020–2021)**: Strategic civil-military relations, aerospace potential cultivation, and community empowerment.
- **Aspers Panglima TNI (2021–2022)**: Senior tri-service human capital advisor to the Commander of the Indonesian Armed Forces (Panglima TNI), overseeing officer promotion boards, career architecture, and personnel policy.
- **Dansesko TNI (2022–2023)**: Command of Indonesia's premier tri-service joint staff college, shaping doctrine, joint operational art, and strategic command education for senior military and police officers.
- **Kepala Badan Nasional Pencarian dan Pertolongan / BASARNAS (2023–2026)**: Cabinet-level agency leadership commanding national search, rescue, and extraction operations across land, maritime, aviation, and disaster domains.

### Tanda Kehormatan Militer & Sipil

Marsdya Kusworo holds fourteen distinguished state honors, including Bintang Dharma, Bintang Yudha Dharma Pratama, Bintang Yudha Dharma Nararya, Bintang Swa Bhuwana Paksa Pratama, Bintang Swa Bhuwana Paksa Nararya, Satyalancana Kesetiaan (VIII, XVI, XXIV, and XXXII Tahun), Satyalancana Dwidya Sistha (with Ulangan I and II), Satyalancana Teladan, and Satyalancana Dharma Dirgantara.

## 🏛️ Strategic & Doctrinal Contributions to P3MDI

On June 22, 2026, during **Phase I (Fondasi Karakter, Kepemimpinan & Kedisiplinan Militer)** of the **Program Presiden untuk Pemimpin Masa Depan (PPPMD / P3MD / P3MDI Batch I Soedirman)** at **Kodiklat TNI Serpong**, Marsdya Kusworo delivered a foundational masterclass titled **"Crisis Leadership: Leading Under Pressure — Decide Fast, Act Right, Save Lives"**.

Addressing the cohort of future state-owned enterprise (BUMN) holding executives, Marsdya Kusworo translated battlefield and rescue heuristics into corporate crisis governance:

1. **The Disaster Equation ($\text{Risk} = \frac{\text{Hazard} \times \text{Vulnerability}}{\text{Capacity}}$)**: Establishing that while natural or macroeconomic hazards and corporate vulnerabilities drive systemic risk upward, executive leadership and institutional operational capacity serve as the decisive denominator that suppresses catastrophic losses.
2. **The 4-Phase Crisis Leadership Engine**: Navigating extreme events through a continuous loop of _Situational Awareness_ (data inputs and drone/ROV reconnaissance), _Risk Assessment_ (analytical filters), _Decision Making_ (funneled tactical choices), and _Command and Control_ (Incident Commander orchestration).
3. **The Tactical Decision Tree (GO, DELAY, MODIFY, ABORT)**: Institutionalizing structured heuristics when operating under the "Hexagon of Chaos" (Uncertainty, Time Pressure, Information Gap, High Risk, Dynamic Situation, Resource Limitation).
4. **The Absolute Priority Hierarchy**: Ordering executive decisions under fire by the immutable triad: **Save Lives $\rightarrow$ Save Rescuers $\rightarrow$ Save Time**.
5. **The Three Pillars of Crisis Leadership**: Synthesizing _Cognitive Capacity_ (absolute calm and adaptability), _Execution Capacity_ (courageous decisions and cross-agency debureaucratization), and _Moral Capacity_ (humanity as the non-negotiable True North).
