## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Perusahaan Listrik Negara (Persero)**, universally known as **PLN**, is Indonesia's state-owned electric power corporation holding a statutory monopoly on national electricity transmission, system distribution, and retail supply. Founded on October 27, 1945, through Government Edict No. 19/SD/1945, PLN serves over 85 million customers across more than 17,000 islands, managing an installed national generation capacity exceeding 70 gigawatts (GW).

Under the corporate restructuring of 2022, PLN transitioned into a modern holding architecture supported by two generation subholdings—**PT PLN Indonesia Power** and **PT PLN Nusantara Power**—alongside specialized subholdings in primary energy management (PLN Energi Primer Indonesia) and telecommunications/EV charging (PLN Icon Plus). Through **PLN Nusantara Power**, the enterprise manages massive power generation fleets spanning Java-Bali, including critical combined-cycle gas and steam turbine (PLTGU) complexes like **UP Muara Karang**, **UP Paiton**, and **UP Gresik**.

PLN operates at the epicenter of Indonesia's national economic security and international decarbonization commitments. It is tasked with executing the national Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP) and the Electricity Supply Business Plan (RUPTL), navigating the delicate decommissioning of legacy coal-fired plants while integrating renewable solar, hydro, geothermal, and green hydrogen power.

### Business Immersion 1 (October 2026): UP Muara Karang Grid Stability & Merit Order

- **Core Operational Dilemma**: Guaranteeing uninterrupted 24/7 electrical baseload and black-start capability for the national capital (VVIP government quarters, international airports, data centers) while managing fuel cost volatility (LNG supply agreements) and strict decarbonization emission targets.
- **The UP Muara Karang Generation Architecture**:
  - _Strategic Location & VVIP Security_: Sited on the northern coast of Jakarta, UP Muara Karang operates combined-cycle gas turbine blocks (Blok 1, 2, and 3) delivering over 2,100 MW directly into the 150 kV and 500 kV Greater Jakarta ring transmission network.
  - _Merit Order & Peaking Dynamics_: Balancing fast-start gas turbines (peakers responding within minutes to sudden grid demand spikes) against cost-effective baseload steam generation.
  - _Co-Firing & Alternative Energy_: Piloting biomass co-firing and industrial cooling water waste-heat optimization to reduce emissions per megawatt-hour.
- **Strategic Alignment with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**:
  - Sustaining universal electrification targets and government-regulated consumer tariffs (_Tarif Tenaga Listrik / TTL_) while managing multi-billion dollar debt servicing obligations and Take-or-Pay (ToP) power purchase agreements with Independent Power Producers (IPPs).
