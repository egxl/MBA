## 👤 Profile & Background

**Jennifer Nash, Ph.D.** is an executive coach, leadership consultant, and speaker serving senior leaders across Fortune 50 enterprises (including Google, ExxonMobil, JP Morgan, Boeing, and Verizon). A former management consultant at Deloitte Consulting, she specializes in executive self-awareness, leadership transitions, and human-centered organizational performance.

---

## 🧠 Core Frameworks & Contributions

### 1. Personal Values & The Diagnostic Crucible (HBR 2020)

In her seminal Harvard Business Review piece (_What Are Your Personal Values?_), Nash formalized two practical diagnostic instruments for executive introspection:

- **The Adapted Wheel of Life**: Structuring Paul J. Meyer's 8-wedge life model into an actionable spider diagram to expose executive domain neglect.
- **The Life Journey Map**: A 4-phase developmental timeline (_Childhood, Adolescence, Early Adulthood, Present_) tracking 7 cognitive/emotional data points per crucible decision to deconstruct subconscious limiting beliefs.
