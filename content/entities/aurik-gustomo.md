## 👤 Executive & Academic Profile

**Prof. Dr. Aurik Gustomo** is **Professor of People Development** and **Dean of the School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung (SBM ITB)**, where he is also one of the school's founders. Nationally and regionally, he serves as **Chairman of the National Executive Board of the Indonesian Association of Faculties of Economics and Business (AFEBI)**, **Member of the AACSB Asia Pacific Advisory Council**, and **Member of the Board of Honor of the Independent Accreditation Agency (LAMEMBA)**.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

Prof. Aurik Gustomo's executive advisory and research center on **human capital management, corporate culture, organizational health, and ambidextrous organizational design**:

1. **Dual-Logic Organizational Design**: Demonstrating that _Efficiency (Exploit)_ and _Growth (Explore)_ require fundamentally opposite structural choices across 12 design dimensions (vertical vs. flat structure, narrow vs. wide span of control, formal compliance vs. entrepreneurial risk-taking), and that sustainable enterprises combine both via **[[concepts/leadership-ambidexterity|Organizational Ambidexterity]]** (structural, contextual, and leadership dimensions).
2. **Diagnostic-First Transformation (OHI)**: Applying the McKinsey **[[concepts/organizational-health-index|Organizational Health Index (OHI)]]** across 3 clusters (_Internal Alignment_, _Quality of Execution_, _Capacity of Renewal_), 9 components, and 33 management practices before restructuring.
3. **Archetype-Strategy Alignment**: Mapping empirical OHI diagnostics onto four distinct organizational archetypes (_Leadership Driven_, _Execution Edge_, _Market Focus_, and _Knowledge Core_) tailored to BUMN and industrial realities.
