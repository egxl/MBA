## 👤 Executive Profile

**Agus Dwi Handaya** (widely known as **ADH**) is the Director of Human Capital at **PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk**, one of Indonesia's largest financial institutions. He is recognized nationally for pioneering the application of financial engineering, data science, and mathematical modeling to enterprise human capital strategy.

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

### 1. PeopleMath Framework

ADH introduced the **PeopleMath** methodology to translate soft organizational behavior concepts into hard corporate finance metrics. His thesis asserts that human capital decisions must be underwritten with the same analytical precision and ROI accountability as multi-trillion rupiah capital expenditures.

### 2. Talent Density & Compounding Enterprise Value

Advocating a power-law perspective on organizational talent, ADH models high-cognition performance as exponential rather than linear:
$$\text{Output} \propto (\text{Talent Density})^{\alpha}, \quad \text{where } \alpha > 1$$
