## 👤 Executive Profile

**Hadjar Seti Adji** is the Director of Human Capital and Transformation (_Direktur Manajemen Sumber Daya Manusia dan Transformasi_) at **[[entities/pt-wijaya-karya|PT Wijaya Karya (Persero) Tbk]] (WIKA)**. Across more than two decades in Indonesia's state-owned engineering, procurement, and construction (**BUMN Karya**) sector—spanning executive transformation leadership at **PT PP (Persero) Tbk**, **PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk**, and **PT Wijaya Karya (Persero) Tbk**—he has built a reputation as an operational trendsetter who translates human capital theory into industrial turnaround execution.

Rather than treating construction contracting as a purely commoditized price war, Hadjar pioneered the adoption of **Building Information Modeling (BIM)**, **Green Building** standards, and **Sustainable Development Goals (SDG)** integration in Indonesian state-owned contractors, demonstrating how technological and cultural transformation shifts an enterprise from cutthroat _Red Ocean_ bidding into differentiated _Blue Ocean_ value creation.

## 🧠 Core Leadership Doctrines

### 1. From Learner to Transformer for Legacy

Delivered alongside **[[entities/agus-dwi-handaya|Agus Dwi Handaya]]** during the **P3MD Batch I Soedirman** PeopleMath executive briefing on August 31, 2026, Hadjar's central thesis bridges individual learning and institutional permanence:

- **Learner**: Cultivating the _Tough & Agile Learner_ discipline ($Vb > Vp$) to absorb new engineering, digital, and governance paradigms faster than industry disruption.
- **Transformer**: Translating personal mastery into organizational systems, cross-functional teams, and operational breakthroughs that elevate enterprise competitiveness.
- **Legacy**: Ensuring that transformation outlives one's personal tenure through institutionalized standards, empowered people, and enduring sector-wide benchmarks.

### 2. Red Ocean to Blue Ocean Industrial Transformation

In BUMN Karya contracting, competing purely on lowest-bid tender pricing traps enterprises in shrinking margins and balance-sheet stress (**Red Ocean**). Hadjar operationalized **Blue Ocean** differentiation by championing **BIM** and **Green Building** capabilities early—converting engineering complexity into higher-margin, lower-rework project delivery.

### 3. Ethical Execution & Purpose-Driven Velocity

Hadjar anchors aggressive innovation in three non-negotiable behavioral maxims:

- **_"Jujur dan amanah, berani mencoba, dan berani bertanggung jawab."_** — Moral integrity creates the courage to innovate rapidly, and accountability preserves institutional honor (mirroring the P3MD ethos _Berani, Cepat, Terhormat_).
- **_"Cara terbaik memprediksi masa depan adalah dengan menciptakan masa depan itu sendiri."_** — Strategic foresight requires proactive system-building rather than passive forecasting.
- **_"Do the best and the best will find its way back to you."_** — Excellence and generosity toward colleagues, regardless of rank, compound into enduring institutional trust.
