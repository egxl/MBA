## 👤 Academic Profile

**Philip Kotler** (born May 27, 1931) is an American marketing author, consultant, and professor emeritus at Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management. Widely revered as the "Father of Modern Marketing", Kotler transformed marketing from a descriptive, peripheral business trade into an empirical, strategic management discipline grounded in economics and behavioral science.

He holds an M.A. in economics from the University of Chicago (studying under Milton Friedman) and a Ph.D. in economics from MIT (under Paul Samuelson). His seminal textbook, _Marketing Management_ (co-authored with Kevin Lane Keller and Alexander Chernev), now in its 16th edition, is the world's most widely adopted graduate marketing text.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Theoretical Breakthroughs

1. **Broadening the Concept of Marketing (1969):** Together with Sidney Levy, Kotler demonstrated that marketing applies beyond commercial goods to non-profit organizations, public institutions, ideas, and societal causes.
2. **The Strategic Marketing Process (STP + 4Ps):** Kotler codified the progression from market research through **Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning (STP)** to tactical execution via the **Marketing Mix (Product, Price, Place, Promotion)**.
3. **Societal Marketing & Marketing 5.0:** Pioneered the transition from product-driven transactions (Marketing 1.0) and customer-centricity (Marketing 2.0) to human-centric purpose (Marketing 3.0), digital connectivity (Marketing 4.0), and technology for humanity (Marketing 5.0).
