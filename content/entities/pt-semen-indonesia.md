## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Semen Indonesia (Persero) Tbk** (IDX: `SMGR`), branded commercially as **SIG**, is Indonesia's largest building materials manufacturer and cement producer. Originally established in Gresik, East Java, on August 7, 1957, as NV Semen Gresik, the enterprise became Indonesia's first state-owned enterprise to list shares on the Jakarta and Surabaya Stock Exchanges in 1991. Through strategic mergers and holding consolidations, SIG united regional champions including Semen Padang, Semen Tonasa, and Thang Long Cement (Vietnam).

A transformative strategic milestone occurred in 2019 when SIG acquired Holcim Indonesia, rebranding it as **PT Solusi Bangun Indonesia Tbk (SBI)**. Positioned within the **[[entities/danantara|Danantara]]** asset management portfolio, SIG commands over 50% of the domestic cement market share with total annual production capacity exceeding 52 million metric tons.

SIG operates advanced integrated manufacturing facilities across the archipelago, including the flagship **SBI Pabrik Narogong** in Bogor, West Java, alongside Tuban, Cilacap, and Lhoknga. The company manufactures diverse hydraulic cement grades, ready-mix concrete, precast building modules, and aggregates. Under mounting global environmental scrutiny, SIG is pioneering industrial decarbonization across Southeast Asia, transitioning from traditional coal firing toward alternative fuels, renewable biomass, and municipal **Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF)**.

### Business Immersion 1 (October 2026): SBI Pabrik Narogong & Circular Decarbonization

- **Core Operational Dilemma**: Navigating structural domestic cement oversupply (national capacity exceeding domestic demand by ~30%) while executing intense thermal energy cost optimization and reducing the clinker-to-cement ratio without compromising structural compressive strength.
- **The SBI Narogong Manufacturing & Decarbonization Engine**:
  - _Thermal Co-Processing & Municipal RDF_: Siting the Nathabumi environmental waste management division to process sorted municipal solid waste from Jakarta and Bogor into Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF), replacing fossil coal in high-temperature rotary kilns ($> 1400^\circ\text{C}$).
  - _Alternative Raw Materials & Clinker Factor Reduction_: Utilizing fly ash, bottom ash (FABA), and granulated blast-furnace slag to produce blended green cements (InterCement), slashing specific carbon emissions ($t\text{CO}_2/t\text{ cement}$) by over 20%.
  - _Bulk Logistics & Coastal Terminal Network_: Coordinating maritime vessel chartering and rail distribution networks to optimize distribution freight costs across inter-island supply chains.
- **Strategic Alignment with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**:
  - Supporting national strategic infrastructure projects (Trans-Java, IKN Nusantara) with guaranteed building material supply while maintaining positive operating margins in a cutthroat, price-sensitive retail bagged cement market.
