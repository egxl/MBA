## 👤 Academic & Professional Profile

**Ira Fachira, S.Si., M.T., Ph.D.** is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung (SBM ITB). She serves as the **Lead Lecturer for MM5001 Marketing Management (MK3)** in the P3MD MBA Program (Direktorat Pendidikan Profesional Berkelanjutan).

Her academic research and executive teaching focus on strategic marketing management, consumer psychology, brand architecture, and service marketing. In her pedagogical approach, Dr. Fachira emphasizes the rigorous synthesis of global marketing theories (Kotler, Almquist, Webster & Wind) with the operational and socio-economic realities of the Indonesian market.

---

## 🧠 Core Teaching Philosophy & Frameworks

Dr. Fachira anchors the MK3 curriculum around four core competencies:

1. **Core Problem Diagnosis**: Uncovering root market bottlenecks beneath surface sales symptoms.
2. **Ethical Marketing Sensitivity**: Navigating consumer privacy, fair pricing, and greenwashing dilemmas.
3. **End-to-End Marketing Planning**: Structuring customer value creation through the 5C environmental framework, rigorous STP segmentation, and integrated 4Ps/7Ps marketing mixes.
4. **Collaborative Executive Defense**: Challenging student syndicates during boardroom cold-calls to defend marketing investments with rigorous unit economics ($\text{CAC}$, $\text{CLV}$, $\text{WTP}$).
