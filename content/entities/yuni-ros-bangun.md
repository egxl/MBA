## 👤 Executive & Academic Profile

**Dr. Ir. Madju Yuni Ros Bangun, MBA** is an Associate Professor of People and Knowledge Management at the School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung (SBM ITB). She is an expert on human capital development, corporate culture transformation, organizational dynamics, and institutional governance within Indonesian state-owned enterprises (BUMN).

Dr. Yuni Ros Bangun earned her doctorate from Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB), where her landmark empirical research established the **LCOP Model (Model Akhir IV)** analyzing the structural relationships between Strategic Leadership, Corporate Culture, Political Behavior, Organizational Capabilities, and Corporate Performance across Indonesian agribusiness state-owned and private plantation conglomerates.

## 🧠 Core Theoretical Models & Research Breakthroughs

### The LCOP Structural Equation Model (BUMN vs. Private Plantations)

In her empirical analysis across Indonesian plantation enterprises (including PT Perkebunan Nusantara / PTPN and leading private holdings), Dr. Bangun proved that:

1. **Full Mediation of Capabilities**: Leadership and corporate culture have zero direct structural link to bottom-line Return on Assets (ROA); their entire performance impact is fully mediated through **Organizational Capabilities** ($\beta = 1.08$).
2. **The Culture Imperative in BUMNs**: In private agribusiness conglomerates, capabilities are driven almost entirely by top-down leadership authority ($\beta = 1.58$), with culture exerting negligible direct impact ($\beta = 0.06$). In state-owned enterprises (BUMN), corporate culture is an indispensable conduit ($\beta = 0.60$) without which leadership vision cannot be converted into operational capabilities.
3. **The Destructive Cost of BUMN Politics**: Political behavior is significantly more prevalent in state-owned enterprises and is **38% more destructive to financial performance** ($-0.94$ in BUMN vs. $-0.68$ in private firms).

$$\mathbf{\text{BUMN Performance}} = 1.08(\text{Capability}) - 0.94(\text{Politics})$$
