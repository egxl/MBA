## Executive & Theorist Profile

**Datuk (Dr.) Nora Abd Manaf** is an internationally recognized human capital strategist, chartered accountant, chartered banker, and corporate board director. Currently serving as **Chairman of Manaf Gardner Associates** and **Independent Non-Executive Director at MBSB Group**, she is best known for her transformative 16-year tenure as **Group Chief Human Capital Officer (CHCO) at Maybank** (2008–2024), where she championed the institutional philosophy of _"Humanising Financial Services."_

Trained originally as a Chartered Accountant in London (practicing in audit and finance) before crossing five industries—including semiconductor manufacturing (**Intel**), private higher education, telecommunications (**Maxis / US West**), and international banking (**Standard Chartered** across Kuala Lumpur, Hong Kong, London, and Jakarta at **PT Bank Permata Tbk**)—her multidisciplinary career embodies non-linear executive agility.

### Academic & Professional Credentials

- **Academic Formation**: Alumna of Harvard Business School (Advanced Management Program), Heriot-Watt University (PhD by Practice), Cornell University (Executive HR), and Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM).
- **Professional Fellowships & Accreditations**: Chartered Accountant, Chartered Banker (Chartered Banker Institute Scotland), Master Professional (IHRP Singapore), Chartered Fellow (CIPD UK), Gallup Certified Strengths Coach, and Black Belt Sustainability Practitioner.
- **Global Honours**: Recipient of the _World Women Legend and Icon Leadership Award (2025)_ and the _Global Women Leadership Lifetime Award (2024)_.

## Core Philosophy & Institutional Contributions

1. **The People Equation & AI Amplification**: Formulated a strategic synthesis rejecting the false binary between human workers and artificial intelligence (_"The future of HR will not be human or AI. It will be human potential, amplified by AI"_), anchoring sustainable growth on [[concepts/the-people-equation|The People Equation]].
2. **Responsible Capitalism**: Rejecting purely extractive shareholder orthodoxy—exemplified by her challenge to Wall Street layoffs—in favor of sustainable profitability built on living wages, human dignity, and revenue-per-employee empowerment.
3. **Maybank Transformation (2008–2024)**: Partnered with then-CEO Tan Sri Abdul Wahid Omar to shatter Maybank's 50-year USD 1 billion Profit Before Tax (PBT) ceiling within two years, while elevating employee engagement from below 70% to above 80% and transforming Maybank from rank #17 to #1 Employer of Choice nationally and globally.
4. **Skills & Capability-Based Organizations**: Pioneering the abandonment of rigid, confining job descriptions in favor of dynamic micro-credentials, multi-income empowerment, and AI-enabled latent skill inventories—eliminating bureaucratic silos without destructive reorganizations.
