## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk** (IDX: `TLKM`, NYSE: `TLK`) is Indonesia's dominant digital telecommunications enterprise and national digital network orchestrator. Tracing its historical corporate lineage to the colonial telegraph services of 1856 and officially formed as a state enterprise in 1965, Telkom has transformed from a traditional fixed-line telephony utility into a diversified digital conglomerate.

Operating under the BUMN sovereign architecture and aligning with **[[entities/danantara|Danantara]]** asset optimization programs, Telkom coordinates Indonesia's digital backbone via its "Five Bold Moves" (5BM) corporate restructuring:

1. **FMC (Fixed-Mobile Convergence)**: Unifying mobile champion **Telkomsel** with nationwide residential broadband service **IndiHome**.
2. **InfraCo**: Fiber-optic network infrastructure commercialization.
3. **Data Center Co (NeutraDC)**: Consolidating domestic and regional enterprise data center assets under **PT Telkom Data Ekosistem (NeutraDC)**.
4. **B2B Digital IT Services**: Cloud, cybersecurity, and enterprise software solutions.
5. **DigiCo**: Venture building and digital consumer platforms.

Through **NeutraDC**, Telkom operates Tier III and Tier IV certified Hyperscale Data Centers (such as **NeutraDC Sentul**, **NeutraDC Cikarang**, and international facilities in Singapore and Batam). These facilities anchor national data sovereignty, artificial intelligence computing capacity, government cloud infrastructure, and international OTT platform caching.

### Business Immersion 1 (October 2026): NeutraDC Sentul & Danantara Asset Synergy

- **Core Operational Dilemma**: Achieving international energy efficiency standards (Power Usage Effectiveness / PUE $< 1.3$) in a tropical climate with high electricity cooling demand, while scaling colocation capacity and migrating legacy enterprise workloads to sovereign cloud infrastructure.
- **The NeutraDC Sentul Technical Architecture**:
  - _High-Density Computing & Tier IV Resiliency_: Redundant dual-path 2N power feeds, dedicated gas/diesel emergency backup generation, and modular server halls engineered for artificial intelligence and hyperscale computing densities ($> 20 \text{ kW/rack}$).
  - _Advanced Cooling Protocols_: Liquid cooling loops, cold/hot aisle containment, and magnetic bearing chillers designed to minimize energy waste.
  - _Danantara Asset Optimization Synergy_: Structuring real estate and digital infrastructure asset-backed partnerships with Danantara Asset Management to monetize idle land holdings and co-invest in regional cloud infrastructure without over-leveraging Telkom's balance sheet.
- **Strategic Alignment with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**:
  - Fulfilling the national digital sovereignty mandate (protecting sensitive state and financial data under Law No. 27/2022 on Personal Data Protection) while competing commercially against global hyperscalers (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Alibaba Cloud).
