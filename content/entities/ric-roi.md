## 👤 Executive Profile

**Dr. Ric Roi** is Professor of Leadership and Organization at **IMD Business School** (Lausanne / Singapore) and the Academic Director of the **IMD Strategic Talent Lab**. A business psychologist, academic, and former global management consulting managing director, Dr. Roi specializes in executive transitions, board-level CEO succession, and top-team performance dynamics.

Before joining IMD, Dr. Roi held academic appointments at Stanford University, the University of San Francisco, and Nagoya University, alongside senior executive roles leading global leadership practices across two management consultancies. He has advised enterprise boards on over 35 CEO succession appointments and guided dozens of multinational executive committees through large-scale strategic pivots.

His corporate advisory portfolio spans global multinationals and Asian regional champions, including **Bank Mandiri** (founding partner BUMN of P3MD), **LEGO**, **Julius Bär**, **Globe Telecom**, **PTT**, **Siam Commercial Bank (SCB)**, **Siam Cement Group (SCG)**, **Sandoz**, **Telenor**, **Jardine Matheson**, and **Minor International**.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

### 1. Leadership Ambidexterity & The AMBIDEX Model

Dr. Roi co-architected IMD's **C-suite Leader AMBIDEX Performance Model**. His core thesis asserts that sustained enterprise relevance requires leaders to operate with dual strategic orientation:
$$\text{Leadership Ambidexterity} = f(\text{Operational Optimization}_{\text{Exploit}}, \ \text{Future Revenue Creation}_{\text{Explore}})$$

Leaders who over-index solely on current margin extraction risk eventual disruption, while those who pursue speculative innovation without operational discipline fail on near-term cash generation.

### 2. The 5 Levels of Impact Continuum

Dr. Roi structures corporate career progression as an expanding radius of organizational accountability—moving from individual self-mastery to enterprise stewardship:

1. **Level 1: Front Line Individuals** — Personal performance, learning agility, and task integrity ($N=1$).
2. **Level 2: Front Line Managers** — Day-to-day coordination, psychological coaching, and operational quality.
3. **Level 3: Mid-Level Managers** — Translating enterprise strategy into departmental execution and building bench talent.
4. **Level 4: Functional Leaders** — Driving enterprise functional capabilities, governance, and cross-silo synergy.
5. **Level 5: Enterprise Leaders** — Shaping enterprise vision, capital portfolio steering, and long-term societal trust.

### 3. Key Publications & Applied Research

- **_Future Ready Talent: Building a Talent Pipeline for Sustained Business Success_** (co-authored with Tania Lennon) — Methodologies for linking executive succession directly to enterprise growth horizons.
- **_From Telco to TechCo: Globe's Dual Transformation_** (IMD Case Study) — Documenting Globe Telecom's transformation from a capital-heavy utility into an agile technology platform.
- **_Ready to Lead: The Case for Integrating Leadership Development and Succession Management_** (IMD Whitepaper).
