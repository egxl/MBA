## 👤 Executive & Institutional Profile

**Amir Arief, SST., SH., M.Si., Ak., CA, CFE.** is the **Director of Anti-Corruption Socialization and Campaign (_Direktur Sosialisasi dan Kampanye Antikorupsi_)** at Indonesia's **Corruption Eradication Commission (_Komisi Pemberantasan Korupsi_ / KPK)**. Combining forensic accounting, legal scholarship, strategic intelligence, and nearly two decades of frontline investigative and preventive service, he leads KPK's national education and integrity-building mandate across ministries, state-owned enterprises (BUMN), defense institutions, and civil society.

His academic credentials span state financial accounting, law, and strategic intelligence: **D-III** and **D-IV Accounting** at **Sekolah Tinggi Akuntansi Negara (STAN)**, a **Bachelor of Laws (S1 Hukum)** from **Universitas Bung Karno Jakarta**, and a **Master of Science (S2) in Strategic Intelligence Studies** at the **School of Strategic and Global Studies (SKSG), Universitas Indonesia**. Professionally, he holds Chartered Accountant (**CA**) and Certified Fraud Examiner (**CFE**) credentials.

## 🧠 Core Doctrine & Frameworks Delivered

During **Fase I** of the **P3MD Batch I Soedirman** program at **Mabes TNI–Kodiklat, Tangerang Selatan** on **June 12, 2026**, Amir Arief delivered the foundational anti-corruption plenary **"Moral Courage & Moral Reasoning"**, equipping future BUMN leaders with five operational pillars:

1. **Empirical Corruption Baseline & Statutory Taxonomy**: Grounding integrity urgency in Indonesia's 2025 CPI (`34/100`), National Integrity Index (`72.32/100`), and the 7 statutory corruption classes across `UU Tipikor` and `UU No. 1/2026 (KUHP)`.
2. **Pentagon of Fraud & Social Cost Iceberg**: Demonstrating how _Pressure, Opportunity, Rationalization, Capability,_ and _Arrogance_ generate explicit, anticipation, reaction, and implicit economic losses that dwarf the stolen cash itself.
3. **Gratification & LHKPN Discipline**: Enforcing `Peraturan KPK No. 2/2019` (_"Tolak! Kalau tidak bisa ditolak, laporkan!"_) and distinguishing asset-filing rates from true verification compliance via `elhkpn.kpk.go.id`.
4. **The Slippery Slope & ABC Cultural Engine**: Exposing how normalized micro-favors (_"everyone does the same"_) escalate into ethical compromise, and how leadership role-modeling reshapes _Attitude $\rightarrow$ Behaviour $\rightarrow$ Culture_.
5. **KPK Trisula Strategy**: Integrating **Education** (_"Don't want to corrupt"_), **Prevention** (_"Can't corrupt"_), and **Enforcement** (_"Dare not corrupt"_) with public participation (`kws.kpk.go.id` / Call Center `198`).
