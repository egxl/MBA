## 🏛️ Central Profiles & Case Subjects

The **Entity Directory** catalogs the principal academic theorists, corporate case subjects, executive leaders, and state-owned enterprises analyzed across the **P3MD MBA** curriculum.

By centralizing case subjects into living entity profiles, case insights from multiple courses (e.g. enterprise analysis spanning corporate strategy, operations, and financial valuation) are consolidated into a comprehensive 360-degree organizational view.

$$\mathbf{THEORISTS \quad \boldsymbol{\cdot} \quad ENTERPRISES \quad \boldsymbol{\cdot} \quad BUMN \ ECOSYSTEM}$$

> [!NOTE] Epistemic Data Provenance: Real-World Identity vs. Case-Basis Snapshots
> Entity profiles in this directory distinguish between **Timeless Real-World Corporate Identity** (verified corporate history, ownership structure, and legal mandates) and **Curriculum Case Snapshots** (quantitative exhibits, unit economics, and student pro-forma projections drawn from classroom case studies and syndicate problem sets). If an illustrative or composite teaching company is indexed, it carries a dedicated `category: fictional-enterprise` badge.

## 👥 Principal Taxonomy

### 1. Academic Theorists & Framework Architects

- **[[entities/philip-kotler|Philip Kotler]]**: "Father of Modern Marketing", STP framework, societal marketing concept, and customer value foundations (Kellogg).
- **[[entities/richard-b-chase|Richard B. Chase]]**: Customer contact approach, service-system design matrix, and service poka-yokes (USC Marshall).
- **[[entities/ric-roi|Dr. Ric Roi]]**: Leadership ambidexterity, the C-suite AMBIDEX model, and 5 Levels of Impact (IMD Business School).
- **[[entities/annie-mckee|Annie McKee]]**: Resonant leadership, emotional contagion, and workplace well-being (PennCLO / Teleos).
- **[[entities/nick-craig-scott-snook|Nick Craig & Scott Snook]]**: Purpose-to-impact planning and authentic leadership (ALI / HBS).
- **[[entities/jennifer-nash|Jennifer Nash]]**: Wheel of Life diagnostic and Life Journey Map (Deloitte / Executive Coach).
- **Clayton Christensen**: Disruptive innovation, resource allocation process, and personal life strategy.
- **Michael Porter**: Competitive strategy, five forces industry analysis, and value chain decomposition.
- **Eliyahu Goldratt**: Theory of Constraints (TOC) and operational bottleneck management.
- **Henry Mintzberg**: Organizational structures, managerial roles, and emergent strategy formation.
