## 👤 Executive & Leader Profile

**Prof. (Hon.) Dr. Fadli Zon, S.S., M.Sc.** serves as **Menteri Kebudayaan Republik Indonesia**—leading Indonesia's first standalone Ministry of Culture since independence. A historian, cultural advocate, and statesman, his mandate elevates national culture from a secondary portfolio under education or tourism into a foundational pillar of statecraft, national identity, and economic diplomacy anchored in **Pasal 32 Ayat (1) UUD 1945** and **Undang-Undang Nomor 5 Tahun 2017** on the Advancement of Culture (_Pemajuan Kebudayaan_).

On Wednesday, November 25, 2026, Dr. Fadli Zon delivered the morning executive plenary masterclass to the **P3MD Batch I Soedirman** cohort at Kampus DCU Nawasena, titled **"Menjadi Pemimpin Teladan di Tengah Masyarakat: Belajar dari Sejarah dan Kearifan Bangsa"**. He challenged future state enterprise (BUMN) executives to anchor strategic decisions in Indonesia's deep civilizational identity rather than viewing transformation solely through imported technocratic templates.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

Dr. Fadli Zon frames culture not as ceremonial entertainment or a passive budget cost, but as durable sovereign capital that outlasts finite extractive commodities such as coal, nickel, oil, and gas:

1. **Cultural Megadiversity & Civilizational Statehood (_Out of Nusantara_)**:
   - Indonesia encompasses **1,340 ethnic groups** and **718 regional languages** (~10% of the world's living languages) alongside foundational paleoanthropological heritage—including over **60%** of global _Homo erectus_ fossil discoveries (e.g., Sangiran's five UNESCO clusters) and the **67,800-year-old** rock art at Liang Metanduno, Muna Island (published in _Nature_, January 2026).
   - Advancing the _Out of Nusantara_ narrative (_reinventing Indonesian identity_), he calls on leaders to dismantle colonial "Far East" mental framing and ground institutional confidence in empirical evidence.
2. **Cultural & Historical Justice via Repatriation**:
   - Stewards systematic repatriation of Nusantara artifacts and fossils held abroad—including the return of more than **30,000 fossils** from the Eugène Dubois collection and ongoing recovery of the 1859 Banjar Diamond (_Berlian Banjar_) and Sangiran collections in Germany—to restore collective historical memory.
3. **Four Pillars of Cultural Policy & 10 Objects of Advancement**:
   - Operationalizes UU No. 5/2017 across four strategic policy pillars: **Perekat Bangsa** (National Cohesion), **Sumber Identitas** (Identity & Character), **Penggerak Ekonomi** (Creative Economy), and **Instrumen Diplomasi** (Cultural Soft Power).
4. **Historical Caution Against Extractive Elites & Character Erosion**:
   - Traces how colonial extraction (VOC 1602, _Politik Etis_, high colonial GDP per capita enjoyed by Europeans while _bumiputra_ remained laborers) and the 1997–1998 IMF-prescribed liberalization eroded strategic national industries.
   - Warns future BUMN leaders of _"the betrayal of the elites"_ and emphasizes the maxim: losing wealth loses nothing, losing health loses something, but **losing character loses everything**.
5. **Value-Based Role Modeling & Field-First Diagnosis**:
   - Advocates Indonesian leadership virtues (**Amanah**, **Gotong Royong**, **Musyawarah**, and **Keteladanan**) paired with the execution triad—_getting motivation right_, _getting arrangement right_, and _getting coordination right_—and listening directly in the field before prescribing policy (_"jangan sampai sakit kepala diberi obat sakit perut"_).
