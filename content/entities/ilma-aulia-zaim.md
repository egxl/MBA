## 👤 Executive Profile

**Ilma Aulia Zaim, Ph.D.** is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung (SBM ITB). She serves on the faculty teaching team for MM5001 Marketing Management in the P3MD Executive MBA program, specializing in tourism marketing, consumer value creation, and brand engagement.

Dr. Zaim earned her doctorate with research focusing on consumer behavior and service experiences. In executive education, she is recognized for integrating qualitative customer empathy ("marketing as an art") with rigorous data-driven analytics ("marketing as a science").

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

1. **Leadership in VUCA Environments:** Dr. Zaim emphasizes that executive marketing leaders must excel at four continuous loops: _Planning $\rightarrow$ Strategize $\rightarrow$ Execute $\rightarrow$ Monitoring_. In volatile, uncertain, complex, and ambiguous markets, rigid 5-year plans fail without rapid sensing mechanisms.
2. **The "Art and Science" of Marketing:** Marketing is neither purely intuitive design nor cold spreadsheet arithmetic. The "art" lies in uncovering unspoken consumer pain points and psychological needs through active listening; the "science" lies in pricing architecture, segmentation sizing, and calculating Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI).
3. **Internal and External Value Alignment:** Commercial success requires marketing to act as the bridge between internal departmental value chains (Operations, HR, Finance) and external stakeholder delivery networks (_warungs_, distributors, digital platforms).
