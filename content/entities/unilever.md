## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**Unilever PLC** is one of the world's largest multinational consumer goods corporations, headquartered in London. Its Indonesian subsidiary, **PT Unilever Indonesia Tbk (IDX: UNVR)**, has operated in the archipelago since 1933, serving as a pillar of Indonesia's modern FMCG and personal care economy.

UNVR operates iconic household brands across beauty, personal care, and home care, including _Rinso_, _Surf_, _Molto_, _Sunlight_, _Pepsodent_, _Lifebuoy_, and _Bango_.

## 🧠 Strategic Challenges & Case Appearances

### 1. Low-Income Mass-Market Entry (Brazil, 1997–2007)

Unilever's expansion into Northeast Brazil is a landmark INSEAD case study in bottom-of-the-pyramid marketing. Unilever confronted the dilemma of defending its premium flagship brand (_Omo_) against cannibalization while engineering a bespoke regional offering (_Ala_) for 48 million hand-washing consumers.

- _Case Study:_ [[courses/mk3-marketing-management/week-01/case-unilever-brazil-low-income|Unilever in Brazil (1997–2007)]]

### 2. Archipelago Mass-Market Defense (Indonesia, 2026)

Facing domestic sales contraction (-8.7% YoY in FY24) and relentless competition from domestic challenger Wings Group (_Daia_, _SoKlin_), UNVR executed a comprehensive "business reset"—divesting its ice cream division, overhauling go-to-market distributor networks, and targeting the 137.5 million-strong "aspiring middle class".

- _Case Study:_ [[courses/mk3-marketing-management/week-01/case-unilever-indonesia-2026|Unilever Indonesia (2026): Winning the Mass Market]]
