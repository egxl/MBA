## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**Unilever PLC** is one of the world's largest multinational consumer goods corporations, headquartered in London. Its Indonesian operating subsidiary, **PT Unilever Indonesia Tbk (IDX: UNVR)**, has operated in the archipelago since 1933, serving as a cornerstone of Indonesia's modern FMCG and personal care economy.

UNVR maintains iconic household brands across beauty, personal care, and home care, including _Rinso_, _Surf_, _Molto_, _Sunlight_, _Pepsodent_, _Lifebuoy_, and _Bango_.

### 2. Archipelago Mass-Market Defense (SBM ITB: Indonesia, 2026)

- **Source Case**: [[courses/mk3-marketing-management/week-01/case-unilever-indonesia-2026|Unilever Indonesia (2026): Winning the Mass Market]]
- **Case Scenario & Pedagogical Data**:
  - **Financial Contraction**: Net sales fell to $\text{IDR } 35.1\text{ trillion}$ ($-8.7\%$ YoY in FY24) with net profit contracting to $\text{IDR } 3.4\text{ trillion}$ amid middle-class down-trading and raw material inflation.
  - **Target Segment**: The 137.5 million-strong "Aspiring Middle Class" ($49.2\%$ of population; per-capita monthly spend of $\text{Rp } 874\text{k}\text{–}\text{Rp } 2.04\text{M}$), demanding high-frequency single-use sachet formats ($\text{Rp } 1,500\text{–}\text{Rp } 2,000$).
  - **Competitive Dilemma**: Rebalancing Modern Trade dominance against the pervasive rural traditional trade (_warung_) network of domestic challenger Wings Group.
