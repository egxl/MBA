## 👤 Profile & Scholarship

**Annie McKee, Ph.D.** is a renowned organizational scholar, executive coach, and Director of the PennCLO Executive Doctoral Program at the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education. She co-founded the Teleos Leadership Institute and is internationally recognized for her seminal research on emotional intelligence, resonant leadership, and executive well-being.

---

## 🧠 Core Frameworks & Contributions

### 1. Resonant Leadership & Workplace Well-Being

In her landmark Harvard Business Review research (_Being Happy at Work Matters_), McKee debunked the traditional corporate myth that "work is not personal":

- **Emotional Contagion**: Leaders infect their organizations with their emotional state; dissonance produces neurological cognitive shutdowns in teams.
- **The Three Pillars of Engagement**:
  1. A compelling personal vision linked to enterprise direction.
  2. A sense of genuine purpose beyond shareholder value.
  3. Resonant, trusting workplace relationships.

### 2. Major Works

- _How to Be Happy at Work: The Power of Purpose, Hope, and Friendship_ (Harvard Business Review Press, 2017)
- _Becoming a Resonant Leader_ (with Richard Boyatzis & Frances Johnston, 2008)
- _Primal Leadership: Unleashing the Power of Emotional Intelligence_ (with Daniel Goleman & Richard Boyatzis, 2002)
