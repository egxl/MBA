## 👤 Executive & Leader Profile

**Prof. Dr. Ir. Tatacipta Dirgantara, M.T., IPU, ASEAN Eng.** is an aerospace and mechanical engineering scholar serving as **Rector of Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) for the 2025–2030 term**. His academic and institutional leadership focuses on bridging university-led scientific research, domestic manufacturing capability, and state-owned enterprise (BUMN) industrial demand to break Indonesia out of the middle-income commodity trap.

On Wednesday, November 25, 2026, Prof. Tatacipta delivered the midday executive plenary masterclass to the **P3MD Batch I Soedirman** cohort at Kampus DCU Nawasena, titled **"Menuju Indonesia yang Mandiri: Peningkatan Kapasitas SDM dan Penguasaan IPTEK bagi Industri Strategis Indonesia"** (also framed around knowledge- and innovation-based leadership for **Indonesia Emas 2045**).

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

Prof. Tatacipta synthesizes industrial macroeconomics, aerospace history, and university-industry technology commercialization into five core leadership doctrines:

1. **Anti-"Silverback Gorilla" Facilitator Leadership**:
   - Rejects the dominant-alpha leadership style in which two strong leaders cannot coexist in one group and instead fracture organizations into silos.
   - Defines modern strategic leadership as ecosystem facilitation: lowering institutional ego to connect universities, research institutes, BUMN operators, and government regulators.
2. **Technology & Total Factor Productivity (TFP) as Prosperity Engine**:
   - Demonstrates empirically that Indonesia's low R&D intensity (**0.28% of GDP** vs. South Korea **5.21%**, Japan **3.40%**, China **2.56%**) and negligible TFP contribution (**~0.1%** vs. China **2.9%**) stem from consumption- and import-led growth rather than technological mastery.
   - Identifies three prerequisites for high-income industrial sovereignty: superior human capital, domestic process/technology mastery, and **~25 years of policy consistency** across administrations.
3. **The B.J. Habibie Reverse-Transformation Model**:
   - Recounts Indonesia's 21-year aerospace trajectory from licensed assembly (`NC-212`) and joint design (`CN-235`) to indigenous fly-by-wire engineering (`N-250`, 1995) and `N-2130` planning before the 1998 IMF interruption—proving that the true output was never merely the aircraft, but human capability, laboratories, and institutions.
4. **Dismantling the "Transfer of Technology" Illusion**:
   - Warns that core industrial technologies are never gifted passively. Nations achieve independence only through **co-design**, **co-manufacturing**, mandatory **industrial offsets** (e.g., China's staged 25%–50%–75%–100% local labor rules; Malaysia's CTRM Airbus A400M offset), and domestic **off-take guarantees**.
5. **Bridging the Valley of Death (TKT 4–6) & Sovereign IP Protection**:
   - Contrasts Indonesia's **Katalis Merah Putih** triumph-and-bottleneck (ITB's 2003–2020 synthesis of palm-to-green-fuel catalysts with Pertamina stalling at commercial refinery trials due to short-term KPI fear) against foreign-funded laboratories in Morowali (**Rp0.5T** lab producing **144 patents/year** captured abroad) and rare-earth collaboration halts, urging BUMN leaders to act as courageous first buyers (_early adopters_) of domestic R&D.
