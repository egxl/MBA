## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**PT Pertamina (Persero)** is Indonesia’s national state-owned energy enterprise (_Badan Usaha Milik Negara / BUMN_), holding statutory responsibility for managing the country's integrated oil, natural gas, and renewable energy resources. Headquartered in Jakarta, Pertamina operates an extensive integrated value chain spanning upstream exploration and production, midstream refining and petrochemicals, and downstream distribution across the Indonesian archipelago.

Under its modern holding-subholding corporate structure, refining and petrochemical manufacturing operations are managed under **PT Kilang Pertamina Internasional (KPI)**, overseeing major national refinery complexes (_Refinery Units / RU_) including Balongan, Cilacap, Dumai, Plaju, Balikpapan, and Kasim. Pertamina plays a pivotal strategic role in safeguarding national energy resilience (_ketahanan energi nasional_) while executing decarbonization roadmaps toward Net Zero Emissions by 2060.

### Case Overview: Sustainable Combustion Technology Selection (MK5 Week 07)

Within the **Pertamina Kilang Nusantara** decision dilemma, the engineering and operational executive team at the Crude Distillation Unit (CDU) faces severe efficiency and emissions constraints:

- **Operational Baseline**: Crude intake expanded from **118 MBSD (2022)** to **132 MBSD (2024)**, increasing fuel gas consumption and stack emissions across fired heaters.
- **Historical Crucible**: A prior 2022 low-$\text{NO}_x$ retrofit failed due to flame instability and tube hot spots, causing operational disruptions and capital write-offs.
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Dilemma**: Management must select an optimal sustainable combustion technology across three competing options:
  1. _Conventional Air Burner_ (Baseline Capex: IDR 180 Billion; proven, low technical risk, standard emissions).
  2. _Regenerative Burner_ (Capex: IDR 240 Billion; flue-gas heat recovery, higher thermal efficiency, moderate emissions cut).
  3. _Oxy-fuel Burner_ (Capex: IDR 300 Billion; high-purity oxygen combustion, near-zero direct flue emissions, highest energy density, higher operational complexity).
- **Analytical Resolution**: Resolved using the [[concepts/analytic-hierarchy-process|Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)]] across 6 primary criteria (Societal Value 37.67%, Environmental Value 29.02%, Economic Value 14.56%, Tech Competitiveness 8.10%, Supply Chain 6.94%, Operational Flexibility 3.71%) with proven mathematical consistency ($CI = 0.0777, CR = 0.0626 < 0.10$).

### Field Immersion Case Snapshot: ITJ Plumpang Downstream Logistics (Business Immersion 1)

During the **[[program/alp/business-immersion-1/pertamina-patra-niaga|Business Immersion 1 (October 2026)]]** field visit, executive participants from Syndicates 9, 11, 12, and 13 analyzed downstream fuel logistics at **PT Pertamina Patra Niaga – Integrated Terminal Jakarta (ITJ Plumpang)**:

- **Strategic Criticality**: ITJ Plumpang supplies over 20% of Indonesia's national fuel requirements and upwards of 70% of Greater Jakarta's daily consumption via pipeline receipts from RU VI Balongan and automated road-tanker loading gantries operating 24/7.
- **Key Diagnostic Dilemma**: Balancing extreme industrial process safety and community buffer-zone risk mitigation with high-throughput tanker dispatch velocity, driver fatigue monitoring, and digitized fuel reconciliation to eliminate distribution losses (_losses threshold < 0.2%_).
- **Synergy with [[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO vs Commercial Duality]]**: Fulfilling the national 24/7 fuel security mandate (_BBM Satu Harga_ and subsidized Solar/Pertalite) under tight fiscal compensation formulas while competing in deregulated commercial retail sectors (Pertamax Series, avtur, industrial bunkering).

### Cohort Diagnostic Census: Downstream Energy Resilience (BIREF5 — November 2026)

Following the plenary address and business immersion briefing on November 11, 2026, the full P3MD cohort ($N=58$ submissions across Groups 1–4) conducted an exhaustive diagnostic on Pertamina Patra Niaga's distribution network:

- **Terminal Automation System (TAS) Resilience**: Over $98.3\%$ of participants emphasized that loading meters and volume reconciliation must maintain automated, edge-computing offline fail-safe modes during WAN network or central server outages to avoid loading freezes.
- **HSSE & Socio-Spatial Buffer Zoning**: Over $98.3\%$ evaluated safety buffer-zone governance, blast mitigation walls, and contractor driver (_Awak Mobil Tangki / AMT_) fatigue management adjacent to dense civilian neighborhoods.
- **Fleet Scheduling & Filling Point Bottlenecks**: Over $94.8\%$ modeled gantry throughput constraints, advocating algorithmic tanker-compartment matching to eliminate retail SPBU stockout risks across Jabodetabek.
- **Subholding Portfolio Synergy**: Evaluated integration between Pertamina Patra Niaga (retail/trading), Pertamina International Shipping (PIS, marine fleet), and Pertamina Energy Terminal (PET, storage infrastructure).
