## 👤 Executive Profile

**Dony Oskaria** is an Indonesian corporate turnaround executive and senior public-sector asset steward serving concurrently as **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** of **[[entities/danantara|BPI Danantara]]** and **Head of Badan Pengaturan BUMN (BP BUMN)** (formerly Vice Minister of BUMN). Positioned at the structural intersection of Indonesia's 2025–2026 state-enterprise reform, he oversees both the regulatory preservation of public-interest mandates and the commercial restructuring of Indonesia's sovereign corporate portfolio.

Before assuming dual sovereign-holding and regulatory leadership, Oskaria built his operational reputation across major hospitality, aviation, and financial turnarounds—most notably serving as Vice President Director of **PT Garuda Indonesia (Persero) Tbk** during its landmark post-pandemic restructuring and subsequently as founding President Director / CEO of **[[entities/injourney|InJourney (PT Aviasi Pariwisata Indonesia)]]** (2021–2024), where he integrated state-owned airports, aviation services, hotels, retail, and cultural tourism destinations into a unified ecosystem.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy & Contributions

### 1. _Transformasi Total Harga Mati_ & Portfolio Rationalization

Oskaria rejects conglomerate expansion measured by subsidiary count or employee headcount. Under his operational stewardship at BPI Danantara, Indonesia initiated a sweeping portfolio compression from **over 1,000 state-owned entities down to roughly 250 focused enterprises**—pruning 652 non-core, loss-making subsidiaries and consolidating fragmented operations across industrial estates (PT Danareksa), national logistics (PT Pos Indonesia), digital infrastructure ([[entities/pt-telkom-indonesia|PT Telkom Indonesia]]), insurance (IFG), and rail systems ([[entities/pt-kereta-api-indonesia|PT KAI]]–PT INKA).

### 2. Dual-Pillar Statutory Governance (`BP BUMN` vs. `BPI Danantara`)

To eliminate historical conflicts where a single ministry acted simultaneously as regulator, shareholder, and operator, Oskaria champions strict operational separation under **Law No. 16 of 2025** (**BP BUMN**, holding the **1% Seri A Dwiwarna** golden share for charter vetoes and **[[concepts/pso-commercial-duality|PSO oversight]]**) and **Law No. 1 of 2025** (**BPI Danantara**, holding **99% Seri B** commercial equity for capital reallocation, M&A, and dividend compounding).

### 3. Directorship as Standard & Chosen Sovereign Responsibility

Oskaria anchors executive accountability in two foundational principles delivered to the **P3MDI Batch I Soedirman** cohort:

- **Capability & Standard**: _"Director is not a title you hold; it is a standard you uphold."_
- **Sovereign Stewardship**: _"It is not a title you hold. It is a responsibility you choose."_

He operationalized these standards through Danantara's **Single Grade System** for meritocratic cross-holding mobility, the **eight BP BUMN Executive Conduct Directives** (zero conflict of interest, strict facility demarcation, anti-hedonism, political neutrality, ban on personal hobby sponsorships, mandatory mentorship, continuous certification, and full BPK audit transparency), and a disciplined **Four-Phase Turnaround Sequencing Model** (Restructuring $\rightarrow$ Culture/People $\rightarrow$ Technology $\rightarrow$ Global Sovereign Value Creation).
