## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**Wings Group** (operating primarily through **PT Sayap Mas Utama** and PT Lion Wings) is one of Indonesia's largest domestic fast-moving consumer goods conglomerates. Founded in Surabaya in 1948 by Johannes Ferdinand Katuari and Harjo Sutanto, Wings began as a cottage soap manufacturer and expanded into a multi-billion dollar FMCG powerhouse.

Wings commands leading market positions across fabric care, personal care, food and beverages (_Mie Sedaap_, _JasJus_, _Ale-Ale_), and packaging. In laundry detergents, its brand portfolio includes **Daia** (mass-market value leader), **SoKlin** (mid-tier fragrance leader), and **Boom** (ultra-budget traditional trade).

---

## 🧠 Competitive Advantage & Operating Philosophy

1. **Unrivaled Traditional Trade (_Warung_) Distribution**: Deep direct-store-delivery (DSD) network reaching millions of neighborhood warungs and kiosks where multinational competitors rely on multi-tier wholesalers.
2. **Accessible Price Architecture**: Formulating detergents to retail at critical psychological price points, leveraging domestic raw material integration to maintain profitability under lean gross margins.
3. **"Lokal Asli Indonesia" Cultural Resonance**: Positioning products around authentic local preference, high-foaming efficacy in well water, and persistent perfume (_wangi_) that resonates with mass-market households.
