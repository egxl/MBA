## 👤 Enterprise Profile

**Zenxin Organic** (operating under Zenxin Agri-Organic Food) is a Malaysian-founded integrated organic food producer, processor, and omnichannel retailer serving Southeast Asia and China. Founded in **2001** in Johor, Malaysia by **Tai Kokkong**, the enterprise initially pioneered Japanese microbial fermentation to convert agricultural waste into organic fertilizer before expanding into palm oil, organic fresh vegetable farming, retail stores, and agro-tourism.

Under Executive Director **Tai Sengyee** (who joined in 2006), Zenxin pursued international organic certification via Australia's **NASAA** (_National Association for Sustainable Agriculture Australia_) alongside **halal certification** for its processed foods, establishing a three-brand portfolio: **Zenxin Organic** (fresh perishable produce), **Simply Natural** (dry organic foods, noodles, teas, oils, and sauces), and **Midori** (organic fertilizers).

### MK 3 (Marketing Management) Week 11: Brick-and-Mortar to Digital Retail Transformation

- **Source Material**: [[courses/mk3-marketing-management/week-11/case-zenxin-organic-digital-retail|Case Study: Zenxin Organic — From Brick and Mortar to Digital Retail]] & [[courses/mk3-marketing-management/week-11/assignment-pre-assignment-zenxin|Week 11 Assignment Debrief]]
- **Operational Footprint (2020 Case Baseline)**:
  - **Farmland & Sourcing**: Over $300\text{ hectares}$ ($360+\text{ acres}$ NASAA-certified including contract farms under the 2008 _Organic Grower Initiative_) across **30 farms** in Malaysia.
  - **Workforce & Retail**: **400 employees**, **14 brick-and-mortar retail stores**, **1 organic recreation park** (Kluang, Johor), plus **Kampong Kampus** ($2.6\text{ ha}$ community campus in Singapore).
  - **Distribution Reach**: Over **200 organic SKUs** distributed through **100+ dealers** into **100+ supermarkets** (Aeon, Giant, Cold Storage, NTUC FairPrice, Village Grocer) across Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong, Cambodia, and Indonesia.
- **Financial & Growth Snapshots**:
  - Sustained an initial **US\$1.64 million loss** during its first three years due to road-transport spoilage (7–9 hour transit) and nascent local demand.
  - Recorded net sales revenue spikes of **19.2% (2017)** and **8.7% (2018)**; recognized by Frost & Sullivan in 2019 as _"The Best Organic Food Company"_ in Malaysia.
- **Digital & CRM Architecture**:
  - Launched home delivery in **2016** and a WordPress/WooCommerce e-commerce store integrated with **[[entities/hubspot|HubSpot CRM]]** in **2018**, alongside a **20,000+ loyal customer database** and **4.1–4.2 / 5.0 Google My Business rating** (versus iHerb Singapore's 2.7).
  - Operated third-party marketplace storefronts on **RedMart**, **Amazon**, **Lazada**, and **HappyFresh** in Southeast Asia, and **WeChat**, **Chilli.com**, and **Taobao** in China.
