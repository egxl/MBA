## 👤 Executive & Biographical Genesis

**Marsekal Muda TNI Dr. Budhi Achmadi, M.Sc.** is a two-star Flag Officer of the Indonesian Air Force (TNI AU), veteran fighter pilot, strategic defense analyst, and **Asisten Strategi Panglima TNI** (Senior Strategic Assistant to the Commander of the Indonesian National Armed Forces at Mabes TNI). Combining frontline tactical aviation command with international defense diplomacy and advanced academic scholarship, Marsda Budhi embodies the modern Indonesian warrior-scholar archetype.

Born in Magetan, East Java, on October 25, 1972—at the doorstep of Lanud Iswahjudi, the premier tactical fighter base of the Indonesian Air Force—Marsda Budhi pursued military aviation through the Indonesian Air Force Academy (_Akademi Angkatan Udara_ / AAU), commissioning with the Class of 1994. He subsequently graduated from the TNI AU Flying School (_Sekolah Penerbang TNI AU_) in 1996 and was assigned to tactical fast-jet operations, piloting the Northrop F-5E/F Tiger II with the storied 14th Air Squadron (_Skadron Udara 14_), which he eventually rose to command.

Alongside his operational fighter commands, Marsda Budhi built extensive academic and defense diplomacy credentials. He completed his Master of Science (M.Sc.) at the prestigious **S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies (RSIS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU)** in Singapore, followed by a doctoral degree (Dr.) from **Universitas Brawijaya (UB)** in Malang. His senior defense credentials include Seskoau (2008), Sesko TNI (2019), and Lemhannas RI (2025).

## 🎖️ Career Appointments & Command Chronology

Marsda Budhi Achmadi's service spans tactical squadron command, international defense diplomacy, joint theater operations, doctrine formulation, and military strategic planning:

- **Komandan Skadron Udara 14**: Command of the F-5E/F Tiger II tactical air superiority squadron at Lanud Iswahjudi, Madiun.
- **Atase Pertahanan (Athan) RI di Brasilia DF**: Defense diplomacy and bilateral defense procurement representation in Brazil.
- **Komandan Lanud Silas Papare (Jayapura)**: Base command overseeing air operations and regional border surveillance across Papua.
- **Komandan Komando Sektor Pertahanan Udara Nasional II (Kosek II) Makassar**: Integrated airspace surveillance and air defense operations across Central and Eastern Indonesia.
- **Wakil Asisten Operasi (Waasops) Panglima TNI**: Joint operational coordination and contingency response planning at Mabes TNI.
- **Kepala Staf Komando Gabungan Wilayah Pertahanan III (Kas Kogabwilhan III) Timika**: Joint operational command overseeing tri-service defense deployments across Papua and Maluku.
- **Panglima Komando Operasi Udara II (Pangkoopsud II) Makassar**: Command of air operations, fighter wings, transport fleets, and forward air bases throughout central and eastern Indonesia.
- **Wakil Komandan Komando Doktrin, Pendidikan, dan Latihan TNI AU (Wadan Kodiklatau)**: Development of curriculum, flight training standards, and officer transformation.
- **Kepala Pusat Pengkajian Strategis, Penelitian, dan Pengembangan TNI (Kapusjianstralitbang TNI)**: Long-range military research, technological foresight, and strategic doctrine formulation for the TNI Commander.
- **Asisten Strategi Panglima TNI**: Principal architect of military strategy, defense capability planning, and strategic resource allocation for the Indonesian Armed Forces.

## 🏛️ Strategic & Doctrinal Contributions to P3MDI

On July 3, 2026, during **Phase 1** of the newly designated **Program Pendidikan Pemimpin Masa Depan Indonesia (P3MDI)** (formerly P3MD, formerly PFLP), Marsda Budhi Achmadi delivered the seminal foundation plenary masterclass **"Kepemimpinan BUMN Abad XXI"** to the P3MDI Batch I Soedirman cohort at **Kodiklat TNI Serpong**.

His core frameworks delivered to the cohort include:

1. **The Thoughts-to-Destiny Feedback Architecture**: Translating Frank Outlaw's maxim (_"Thoughts $\rightarrow$ Words $\rightarrow$ Actions $\rightarrow$ Habits $\rightarrow$ Character $\rightarrow$ Destiny"_) into institutional state enterprise reality: the personal character and disciplined habits of young executives directly determine the geopolitical and economic destiny of Indonesia's BUMNs.
2. **The 12 Public Administration Paradigms**: Historicizing public management from Woodrow Wilson's 1887 dichotomy to contemporary Digital Era Governance, anchoring BUMN reform in Osborne & Gaebler's New Public Management paradigm (_"Steering rather than rowing"_).
3. **The Dual-Mandate Constitutional Matrix**: Reconciling the public welfare mandate of Pasal 33 UUD 1945 with commercial productivity, integrity benchmarks (KPK), and performance-driven talent systems.
4. **Command Red Flags & 6 Leadership Principles**: Synthesizing military command accountability (_Kepemimpinan Militer_ by Letjen TNI Purn. Prabowo Subianto) with selfless corporate stewardship (_tulus, ikhlas, dan tanpa pamrih_).
