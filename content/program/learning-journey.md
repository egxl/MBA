# Learning Journey & 70–20–10 Architecture

The P3MD MBA learning architecture is designed by **SBM ITB** to integrate curriculum, case-based methods, real-world business intervention, layered mentorship, and residential campus immersion into an organic transformational engine.

---

## 1. Five Integrated Components for Transformation

_An integrated 5-component ecosystem designed to transform participant potential into strategic business leaders capable of high-stakes decision-making and disciplined execution._

### Component 1: Desain Kurikulum (_3 Learning Building Blocks_)

- **Block 1 — Foundation Learning**: Learning agility, executive communication, professional presence, strategic thinking, foundational accounting, and management principles.
- **Block 2 — 6 Core Courses**: Rigorous coursework across 6 strategic enterprise disciplines (OB & Managing People, Financial Management & Strategy, Marketing Management, Operations & Supply Chain, Decision Making & Negotiation, Business Analytics) contextualized across 6 primary BUMN sectors.
- **Block 3 — Managerial, Leadership, Entrepreneurship**: Developing managerial execution, enterprise leadership, and entrepreneurial value creation.

### Component 2: Metode Pembelajaran (_Case-Based & Blended Exposure_)

- **Core Method**: Harvard Business School case studies, intensive group problem-solving, behavioral simulations, competitive business gamification, and structured reflection sessions.
- **Learning Reinforcement**: Asynchronous pre-learning on LMS, weekly study planning and retrospective debriefs, BUMN CEO Leader Talks, business sharing circles, and executive company visits.
- **Pedagogical Perspective**: Synthesis of _Academic Rigor + Business Relevance + National Strategic Context + Global Competitiveness_.

### Component 3: Action Learning Project (_Real BUMN Business Challenges_)

Direct deployment of participant teams to solve high-priority strategic bottlenecks in partner state-owned enterprises (BUMN) with measurable ROI and SMART-gated accountability ($Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-Bound$).

### Component 4: Sistem Mentoring (_5-Tier Support Architecture_)

1. **SBM ITB Faculty**: Academic depth, analytical rigor, and foundational theories.
2. **Mentor Bisnis BUMN**: Realities of industrial execution, regulatory dynamics, and market conditions.
3. **Business Coaches**: Refinement of executive thought process, cognitive agility, and leadership growth.
4. **Pengasuh**: Instilling discipline, moral fortitude, ethical leadership, and behavioral consistency.
5. **Project Owners (BUMN Sponsors)**: Ensuring project outputs directly address core enterprise priorities.

### Component 5: Operasional & Fasilitas (_Living-Learning Infrastructure_)

- **Program Operations**: Centralized scheduling, cohort logistics, academic facilitators, LMS management, and performance tracking.
- **Nawasena Living-Learning Environment**: Comprehensive residential facilities at Kampus DCU Nawasena (academic auditoriums, executive library, 24-hour medical clinic, dedicated nutritionists, counseling, wellness & sports facilities, and accommodation).

## 2. The 70–20–10 Learning Allocation Model

The curriculum departs from traditional lecture-heavy formats, deploying a rigorous **70–20–10 adult learning model** calibrated to build instinctive strategic decision-making:

```
                  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │        10% FORMAL LEARNING              │
                  │             (Structuring)               │
                  │   Theoretical foundation & models       │
                  └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                       │
                  ┌────────────────────▼────────────────────┐
                  │        20% SOCIAL LEARNING              │
                  │            (Interacting)                │
                  │  Peer dialogue, feedback & mentorship   │
                  └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                       │
                  ┌────────────────────▼────────────────────┐
                  │      70% EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING          │
                  │               (Doing)                   │
                  │ Real BUMN decisions & tangible impact   │
                  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Cohort Scaling Architecture: From Plenary to Individual

Learning experiences narrow deliberately from large-cohort masterclasses to individual mastery:

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                   COHORT (399 Participants)                 │
  │     Executive Seminars • CEO BUMN Guest Lectures • Panels   │
  └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                 │
  ┌──────────────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
  │            KELAS PARALEL (8 Classes @ 40–70 Participants)   │
  │    Disciplinary Lectures • Case Debriefs • Business Games   │
  └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                 │
  ┌──────────────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
  │             SMALL GROUP (40 Groups @ ~10 Participants)      │
  │     Action Learning Projects • 3x Coaching • 3x Mentoring   │
  └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                 │
  ┌──────────────────────────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
  │                 INDIVIDUAL (399 Participants)               │
  │     Self-Study • Modular Assessments • Individual Reports   │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Cohort Level (399 participants)**: Macro-strategic exposure through plenary sessions, BUMN CEO guest lectures, executive panel discussions, and cohort-wide reflection.
- **Parallel Class Level (8 classes @ 40–70 participants)**: Deep academic dive into the 6 core business disciplines, Harvard case debriefs, and simulation exercises.
- **Small Group Level (40 groups @ ~10 participants)**: Hands-on execution team for the Action Learning Project (ALP) and Social Project, supported by 3 structured business coaching and 3 leadership mentoring sessions.
- **Individual Level**: Personal mastery demonstrated through pre-learning preparation, modular quizzes, problem sets, and individual synthesis reports.
