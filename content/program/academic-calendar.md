## 2. Key Academic & Executive Milestones

### Month 1 (Weeks 1 – 4): Foundations & Framing

- **Week 1 (M1)**: Academic semester commencement. Orientation at Kampus DCU Nawasena.
- **Week 2 (M2)**: **ALP Kick-Off** — Initial scoping with BUMN Project Owners.
- **Week 3 (M3)**: **BUMN CEO Guest Lecture 1** & **BUMN Practitioner Mentoring 1** (Framing alignment).
- **Week 4 (M4)**: **SBM ITB Academic Coaching 1** (Methodology and data gathering design).

### Month 2 (Weeks 5 – 8): Data Deep Dive & Mid-Term Integration

- **Week 5 (M5)**: **Business Immersion 1** (On-site enterprise diagnostic visit).
- **Week 6 (M6)**: **ALP Milestone 1 (M1)**: Formal defense of problem definition, market sensing, and fact pack.
- **Week 7 (M7)**: **BUMN Practitioner Mentoring 2** & **MK4 OSCM Gamification Session** (Supply chain simulation).
- **Week 8 (M8)**: **MID-TERM EXAMINATIONS** across core courses; **BUMN CEO Guest Lecture 2** & **Academic Coaching 2**.

### Month 3 (Weeks 9 – 12): Analysis, Strategy & Solution Design

- **Weeks 9–10 (M9–M10)**: Core strategy formulation, business model innovation, and financial engineering.
- **Week 11 (M11)**: **Business Immersion 2** (Field validation of solutions) & **BUMN Practitioner Mentoring 3**.
- **Week 12 (M12)**: **ALP Milestone 2 (M2)**: Comprehensive defense of solution architecture and financial ROI models; **Academic Coaching 3**.

### Month 4 (Weeks 13 – 16): Synthesis, Social Impact & Final Defense

- **Weeks 13–15 (M13–M15)**: **Social Project Execution** — Applying leadership and managerial skills to community-level initiatives.
- **Week 14 (M14)**: **FINAL TERM EXAMINATIONS**; **MK3 Marketing Gamification**; **BUMN CEO Guest Lecture 3**. Completion of 14-week core lecture series.
- **Weeks 15–16 (M15–M16)**: **ALP Milestone 3 (M3) Final Defense** — High-level capstone presentation to the joint board of BUMN leadership, ministry executives, and SBM ITB examiners.
