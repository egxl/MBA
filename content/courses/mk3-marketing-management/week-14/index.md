# Week 14: Special Topic: Guest Lecture & Marketing Capstone

## 🎯 Executive Summary

The closing week of MK 3 brings industry reality into the classroom through an **Executive Seminar & Guest Lecture from a prominent BUMN CEO / CMO**, followed by the synthesis of the 14-week Marketing Plan design framework.

---

## 1. Executive Guest Lecture Insights: Marketing in BUMNs

### Real-World Strategic Trade-Offs

- Navigating the twin mandates of commercial profitability and public service obligation (PSO).
- Building consumer brand trust in national critical infrastructure (energy, telecommunications, financial services, logistics).
- Managing aggressive digital transformation while maintaining universal service access across the Indonesian archipelago.

---

## 2. MK 3 Capstone Synthesis: The End-to-End Marketing Plan

A comprehensive Marketing Plan integrates every module mastered over the 14-week semester:

```
  1. Executive Summary & Market Sensing (Macro PESTLE & Competitor Scan)
  2. Customer Insights & Behavioral Segmentation (MASDA Criteria)
  3. Strategic Targeting & Perceptual Positioning (POP / POD Definition)
  4. Product & Service Architecture (Value Augmentation & Service Logic)
  5. Value-Based Pricing Strategy (EVC Formulation & Margin Defense)
  6. Omnichannel Distribution Architecture (D2C & Retail Routing)
  7. Integrated Marketing Communications Campaign (Push / Pull Mix)
  8. Brand Equity Governance & Financial ROI Metrics (CLV / CAC Realization)
```
