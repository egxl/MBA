# Week 01: Creating Customer Value & Engagement

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Marketing is not merely advertising or selling; it is the organizational philosophy and process of creating, communicating, and delivering superior customer value in exchange for captured enterprise value (Customer Lifetime Value).

---

## 1. Core Frameworks & Theoretical Foundations

### The Customer Value Equation

$$\text{Customer Perceived Value (CPV)} = \text{Total Customer Benefit} - \text{Total Customer Cost}$$

- **Total Customer Benefit**: Product performance, service reliability, personal status/brand affinity, and emotional resonance.
- **Total Customer Cost**: Monetary price, time expenditure, cognitive search costs, and operational switching friction.

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

The present value of all future cash flows attributed to the customer relationship:
$$\text{CLV} = \sum_{t=0}^n \frac{(p_t - c_t) \cdot r_t}{(1 + d)^t} - \text{CAC}$$
Where $p_t$ = Revenue per customer, $c_t$ = Cost to serve, $r_t$ = Retention rate, $d$ = Discount rate, $\text{CAC}$ = Customer Acquisition Cost.

- **Golden Rule of Unit Economics**: $\frac{\text{CLV}}{\text{CAC}} \ge 3.0$, with CAC payback under 12 months.
