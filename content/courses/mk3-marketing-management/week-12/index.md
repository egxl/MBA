# Week 12: Marketing Mix: Integrated Marketing Communications (Promotion)

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Promotion represents the voice of the brand. **Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC)** coordinates all touchpoints—paid advertising, public relations, personal selling, sales promotion, and social channels—to deliver a clear, consistent, and compelling message across the customer lifecycle.

---

## 1. The Promotional Mix & Channel Coordination

### The 5 Core Promotional Tools

1. **Advertising**: Any paid form of non-personal presentation and promotion of ideas, goods, or services by an identified sponsor (TV, billboards, search engine marketing, digital display).
2. **Sales Promotion**: Short-term incentives to encourage immediate trial or purchase (coupons, discounts, point-of-sale displays, seasonal contests).
3. **Public Relations (PR)**: Building good relations with company publics by obtaining favorable publicity, managing rumors, and corporate storytelling.
4. **Personal Selling**: Personal customer interactions by the firm's sales force for the purpose of making sales and building relationships.
5. **Direct & Digital Marketing**: Direct engagement with carefully targeted consumers for immediate response and long-term engagement (email marketing, performance ads, influencer partnerships).

### The Push vs. Pull Promotional Strategy

- **Push Strategy**: Directing marketing efforts (trade sales promotions, personal selling) toward channel members (wholesalers, retailers) to induce them to carry and push the product to end consumers.
- **Pull Strategy**: Directing marketing efforts (mass consumer advertising, digital campaigns) toward final consumers to induce them to demand the product from retailers, pulling it through the channel.
