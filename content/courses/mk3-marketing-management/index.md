## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Core Problem Diagnosis**: Identify and analyze the core marketing dilemmas and commercial bottlenecks in complex business cases.
2. **Ethical Marketing Sensitivity**: Identify ethical marketing challenges, consumer privacy issues, and fair competitive practices in modern business environments.
3. **Marketing Plan Design**: Take proactive initiative in conceptualizing, structuring, and designing an end-to-end strategic Marketing Plan.
4. **Collaborative Plan Evaluation**: Collaborate effectively in cross-functional groups to evaluate, critique, and optimize strategic marketing proposals.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The curriculum starts with customer value creation and the macro/micro marketing environment, develops customer and market insight through intelligence systems and formal market research, and then uses segmentation, targeting, positioning (STP), and a competitive selling simulation game to frame strategic choices. Weeks 9–12 cover the four marketing mix levers (Product, Price, Place/Digital, and Promotion), culminating in strategic brand equity and an executive guest lecture in Weeks 13–14.
