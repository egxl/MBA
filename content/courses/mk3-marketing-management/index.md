## 🏛️ Syllabus Reference & Academic Governance

<div class="mk-governance-grid">

  <!-- Left Card: Learning Outcomes -->
  <div class="mk-gov-card">
    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Core Problem Diagnosis</strong>: Identify and analyze core commercial bottlenecks and strategic marketing dilemmas in complex business situations.</li>
      <li><strong>Ethical Marketing Sensitivity</strong>: Identify ethical marketing challenges, consumer privacy issues, and fair competitive practices in modern business environments.</li>
      <li><strong>Marketing Plan Design</strong>: Take proactive initiative in conceptualizing, structuring, and designing an end-to-end strategic Marketing Plan.</li>
      <li><strong>Collaborative Plan Evaluation</strong>: Collaborate effectively in cross-functional groups to evaluate, critique, and optimize strategic marketing proposals.</li>
    </ol>
