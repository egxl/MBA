# Week 12: Working Capital Management & Liquidity Strategy

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Profitable firms can easily go bankrupt if they run out of cash. **Working Capital Management** balances the trade-off between operational liquidity (minimizing bankruptcy and stockout risks) and asset profitability (minimizing idle, non-earning current assets).

---

## 1. Core Frameworks & Methodologies

### The Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)

The timeline required to convert cash invested in raw materials back into cash collected from customer sales:

$$\text{CCC} = \text{Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)} + \text{Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)} - \text{Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)}$$

```
Purchase Raw Materials         Pay for Materials             Sell Finished Goods             Collect Cash
         │                             │                              │                           │
         ▼                             ▼                              ▼                           ▼
         ├─────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤                           │
         │           DIO (Operating Cycle Part 1)                     │                           │
         ├─────────────────────────────┤                                                          │
         │      DPO (Payables)         │                                                          │
         └─────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┤
                                       │                      C C C                               │
                                       ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
                                       │                              ├───────────────────────────┤
                                       │                              │     DSO (Receivables)     │
```

- **Aggressive Working Capital Strategy**: Compressing DIO and DSO while extending DPO. Negative CCC (e.g., Dell, Amazon, Walmart) implies suppliers finance the operating assets.
- **Conservative Working Capital Strategy**: Maintaining large safety stocks, generous credit terms to customers, and minimal short-term debt to eliminate liquidity shocks.

### Cash & Treasury Management Models

- **Baumol-Allman Cash Balance Model**: Adapting EOQ mechanics to cash holdings ($C^* = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot T \cdot F}{r}}$).
- **Miller-Orr Model**: Stochastic cash flow management with upper and lower control limits.

---

## 2. Executive Takeaways

- Receivables factoring, reverse factoring (supply chain financing), and dynamic discounting.
- Working capital optimization as a rapid source of internal cash flow during economic downturns.
