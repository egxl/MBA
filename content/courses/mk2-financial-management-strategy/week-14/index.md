# Week 14: Group Project Presentations & Financial Ethics

## 🎯 Executive Summary

The capstone of MK 2 synthesizes technical modeling, strategic valuation, and corporate governance into high-stakes executive group presentations. Participants defend capital allocation recommendations while confronting the critical ethical responsibilities inherent in corporate finance leadership.

---

## 1. Ethical Governance in Corporate Finance

### Ethical Failures & Systemic Consequences

- **Financial Engineering vs. Real Value Creation**: Manipulating earnings (cookie-jar reserves, off-balance-sheet vehicles, aggressive revenue timing) destroys institutional credibility and capital market trust.
- **Fiduciary Responsibility**: The sacred duty of loyalty and prudence owed by directors and CFOs to capital providers and state stakeholders.
- **Conflict of Interest & Insider Trading**: Protecting information integrity and market fairness.

### Ethical Decision-Making Framework for CFOs

1. **Legality**: Does the action comply with the letter and spirit of law (OJK, IDX, tax code)?
2. **Transparency & Disclosure**: Would you be comfortable explaining this accounting treatment on the front page of major financial press?
3. **Long-Term Consequence**: Does this decision sacrifice the enterprise's long-term health for short-term bonus metrics?
4. **Fairness**: Are minority shareholders and non-executive employees being disenfranchised?

## 2. MK 2 Capstone Synthesis: The Integrated Financial Decision

Review of the 14-week corporate finance journey:

1. Understanding the finance function, agency theory, and corporate objectives.
2. Mastering statement analysis, Dupont decomposition, and 3-statement forecasting (EFN).
3. Cost behavior, CVP, breakeven, and Activity-Based Costing (ABC).
4. Time Value of Money, bond duration, and equity valuation models.
5. Capital budgeting decision rules (NPV, IRR, PI), cash flow mechanics, and real options.
6. Optimal payout policy, debt vs. equity financing, and capital structure optimization.
7. Working capital velocity (CCC) and corporate derivative risk governance.
