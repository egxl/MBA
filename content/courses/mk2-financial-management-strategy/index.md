## 🏛️ Syllabus Reference & Academic Governance

<div class="mk-governance-grid">

  <!-- Left Card: Learning Outcomes -->
  <div class="mk-gov-card">
    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Financial Management Function</strong>: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the financial management function and the firm's strategic financial goals.</li>
      <li><strong>Statement Analysis &amp; TVM</strong>: Conduct analytical reviews of financial statements and financial projections; apply time-value-of-money concepts and valuation techniques.</li>
      <li><strong>Core Financial Decisions</strong>: Apply advanced tools to analyze corporate financial decisions—investment (capital budgeting), financing, payout policies, and working capital management—to address complex corporate dilemmas.</li>
      <li><strong>Environment &amp; Ethics</strong>: Identify macroeconomic financial landscapes; champion the paramount importance of ethics and governance in financial decision-making.</li>
    </ol>
