## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Financial Management Function**: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the financial management function and the firm's strategic financial goals.
2. **Statement Analysis & TVM**: Conduct analytical reviews of financial statements and financial projections; apply time-value-of-money concepts and valuation techniques.
3. **Core Financial Decisions**: Apply advanced tools to analyze corporate financial decisions—investment (capital budgeting), financing, payout policies, and working capital management—to address complex, real-world corporate issues.
4. **Environment & Ethics**: Identify the macroeconomic financial environment and institutional landscape; recognize and champion the paramount importance of ethics and governance in financial decision-making.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The 14-week sequence begins with financial foundations and financial statement analysis, then introduces cost/managerial accounting and time value of money before moving directly into the core corporate financial decision modules (capital budgeting, payout, financing, and working capital). Financial risk governance in Week 13 and executive group project presentations in Week 14 complete the progression with ethical synthesis.
