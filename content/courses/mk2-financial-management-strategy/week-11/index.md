# Week 11: Financing Decisions & Financial Environment

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Capital structure strategy requires navigating external financial markets and banking institutions. Executives must choose between bank credit facilities, corporate bond issuances, syndicated loans, convertible instruments, and public equity offerings based on cost, maturity matching, and covenant constraints.

---

## 1. Capital Markets & Financing Instruments

### The Corporate Debt Spectrum

- **Commercial Bank Credit**: Revolving credit facilities (revolvers) and term loans. Fast execution, relationship-based, but burdened by strict financial covenants.
- **Syndicated Loans**: Multi-bank consortia pooling risk for large-scale enterprise financing.
- **Corporate Bond Markets**: Publicly traded debt or private placements. Fixed coupon vs. floating rates, investment-grade vs. high-yield.
- **Sukuk (Islamic Bonds)**: Asset-backed or asset-based financing complying with Shariah principles (Ijarah, Mudarabah, Murabahah). Critical in Indonesian and Southeast Asian capital markets.

### Debt Covenants & Agency Costs of Debt

- **Negative Covenants**: Restrictions on taking on additional senior debt, selling critical operating assets, or paying dividends exceeding agreed ratios.
- **Affirmative Covenants**: Maintaining minimum interest coverage ratios, minimum net working capital, and providing audited financial statements.

### Equity Underwriting & IPOs / Rights Issues

- **Initial Public Offerings (IPOs)**: Underpricing phenomenon, bookbuilding mechanisms, regulatory compliance with OJK and IDX.
- **Seasoned Equity Offerings (SEOs) & Rights Issues**: Preemptive rights allowing existing shareholders to avoid dilution.

---

## 2. The Financial Environment & Macro Drivers

- Yield curve dynamics and central bank monetary policy shifts (BI-Rate, Fed Funds Rate).
- Sovereign risk ratings, country risk premiums, and currency hedging for foreign-denominated debt.
