    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Theoretical Mastery</strong>: Demonstrate a rigorous understanding of operational theories, analytical models, and supply chain reasoning applied to complex industrial contexts.</li>
