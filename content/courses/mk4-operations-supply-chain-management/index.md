## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Theoretical Mastery**: Demonstrate a rigorous understanding of operational theories, analytical models, and supply chain reasoning applied to complex industrial contexts.
2. **Critical Analysis & Managerial Recommendations**: Critique existing operational bottlenecks, process designs, and supply chain models to formulate actionable managerial recommendations.
3. **Global Context**: Understand and leverage the strategic role of operations and supply chain management within the dynamic global business environment.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The course is grouped into four sequential operational layers:

- **Weeks 1–4 (Strategy & Product Design)** align operational strategy with product, service, and capital project design.
- **Weeks 5–7 (Capacity & Process)** examine process flows, strategic capacity planning, queuing theory, and discrete simulation, featuring the **OSCM Gamification Simulation in Week 7**.
- **Weeks 8–10 (Quality Assurance)** provide three rigorous methodologies for quality excellence: Lean Operations, Lean Six Sigma, and Statistical Quality Control (SQC).
- **Weeks 11–14 (Demand Fulfillment)** integrate demand forecasting, Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP), inventory policies, and enterprise systems (MRP and ERP).
