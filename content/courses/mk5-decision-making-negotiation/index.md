## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Core Problem Diagnosis**: Identify and rigorously analyze root problems within complex business cases.
2. **Multiple Perspective Integration**: Build holistic perspectives by synthesizing divergent factors, functional constraints, and contextual socio-political information.
3. **Solution Option Formulation**: Develop creative, viable strategic solution options grounded in evidence and institutional vision.
4. **Structured Decision-Making**: Execute robust decisions using qualitative and quantitative analytical frameworks.
5. **Team Collaboration**: Collaborate effectively and empathetically across multi-stakeholder and cross-functional teams.
6. **Conflict Resolution**: Apply conflict management frameworks to preempt and resolve interpersonal and strategic friction.
7. **Constructive Feedback**: Deliver clear, actionable, and non-defensive feedback in high-pressure group environments.
8. **Persuasive Influence**: Communicate persuasively to influence, align, and convince stakeholders and negotiation counterparts.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The course first establishes negotiation foundations and the rational decision-making framework, then devotes four intensive weeks to **Kepner-Tregoe methodologies**, including structured Decision Analysis tools: **SMART**, **AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process)**, and **Decision Trees**. Two weeks of **Scenario Planning and Systems Thinking** expand executive perspectives to navigate macroeconomic volatility. The final six weeks transition from distributive bargaining dilemmas and value-claiming tactics ("slicing the pie") to integrative strategy, trade-off expansion, and high-stakes multiparty deal architecture.
