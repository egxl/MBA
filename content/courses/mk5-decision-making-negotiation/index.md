## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Core Problem Diagnosis**: Identify and rigorously analyze root problems within complex business cases using structured rational frameworks.
2. **Multiple Perspective Integration**: Build holistic perspectives by synthesizing divergent factors, functional constraints, and contextual socio-political information.
3. **Solution Option Formulation**: Develop creative, viable strategic solution options grounded in evidence and institutional vision.
4. **Structured Decision-Making**: Execute robust decisions under uncertainty using qualitative and quantitative analytical frameworks.
5. **Team Collaboration**: Collaborate effectively and empathetically across multi-stakeholder and cross-functional teams.
6. **Conflict Resolution**: Apply principled negotiation and conflict management frameworks to preempt and resolve interpersonal and strategic friction.
7. **Constructive Feedback**: Deliver clear, actionable, and non-defensive feedback in high-pressure group environments.
8. **Persuasive Influence**: Communicate persuasively to influence, align, and convince stakeholders and negotiation counterparts.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The course first establishes cognitive foundations and the rational decision-making framework, contrasting intuitive heuristics against deliberative analytical rigor. It explores institutional decision making through BUMN corporate transformation cases before deploying structured rational methodologies: **[[concepts/kepner-tregoe-problem-analysis|Kepner-Tregoe Problem Analysis]]**, **[[concepts/value-focused-thinking|Value-Focused Thinking (VFT)]]**, and multi-criteria decision analysis tools including **[[concepts/simple-multi-attribute-rating-technique|SMART]]**, **[[concepts/decision-tree-analysis|Decision Trees under Uncertainty]]**, and the **[[concepts/analytic-hierarchy-process|Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)]]**. The curriculum culminates in strategic negotiation, shifting from distributive bargaining tactics to integrative value creation and multiparty deal structuring.
