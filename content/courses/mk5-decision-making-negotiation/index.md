## 🏛️ Syllabus Reference & Academic Governance

<div class="mk-governance-grid">

  <!-- Left Card: Learning Outcomes -->
  <div class="mk-gov-card">
    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Core Problem Diagnosis</strong>: Identify and rigorously analyze root problems within complex business cases using structured rational frameworks.</li>
      <li><strong>Multiple Perspective Integration</strong>: Build holistic perspectives by synthesizing divergent factors, functional constraints, and contextual socio-political information.</li>
      <li><strong>Solution Option Formulation</strong>: Develop creative, viable strategic solution options grounded in evidence and institutional vision.</li>
      <li><strong>Structured Decision-Making</strong>: Execute robust decisions under uncertainty using qualitative and quantitative analytical frameworks.</li>
      <li><strong>Team Collaboration</strong>: Collaborate effectively and empathetically across multi-stakeholder and cross-functional teams.</li>
      <li><strong>Conflict Resolution</strong>: Apply principled negotiation and conflict management frameworks to preempt and resolve friction.</li>
      <li><strong>Constructive Feedback</strong>: Deliver clear, actionable, and non-defensive feedback in high-pressure group environments.</li>
      <li><strong>Persuasive Influence</strong>: Communicate persuasively to influence, align, and convince stakeholders and negotiation counterparts.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
