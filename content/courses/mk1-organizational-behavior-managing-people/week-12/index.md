# Week 12: Performance Management, Compensation, and Benefit

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Performance management translates enterprise strategy into individual operational priorities. When coupled with transparent, equitable compensation architecture (Total Rewards), it aligns executive incentives with long-term shareholder and stakeholder value creation.

---

## 1. Core Frameworks & Theoretical Foundations

### Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) vs. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **KPIs (Run the Business)**: Health metrics, operational baselines, and SLA compliance (e.g., uptime, margin maintenance, safety rates).
- **OKRs (Change the Business)**: Ambitious, qualitative objectives paired with aggressive, quantifiable key results to drive strategic breakthroughs.

### Total Rewards Architecture

1. **Fixed Compensation**: Base pay reflective of market parity, job scope, and technical complexity.
2. **Short-Term Incentives (STI)**: Annual bonuses tied to operational P&L performance, EBITDA targets, and personal milestone achievement.
3. **Long-Term Incentives (LTI)**: Equity participation, phantom stock, or multi-year performance cash awards designed to align executives with enterprise compounding over 3–5 year horizons.
4. **Benefits & Well-being**: Health, retirement, executive physicals, and family security.
5. **Recognition & Development**: Intangible status rewards, executive education sponsorship.

---

## 2. Executive Takeaways

- De-linking developmental feedback from annual salary reviews to foster genuine growth dialogues.
- Designing executive compensation governance to deter reckless short-term risk-taking.
