## 🏛️ Syllabus Reference & Academic Governance

<div class="mk-governance-grid">

  <!-- Left Card: Learning Outcomes -->
  <div class="mk-gov-card">
    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Collaborative Capability</strong>: Understand how to collaborate effectively with diverse parties across functional boundaries.</li>
      <li><strong>Conflict Management</strong>: Apply conflict resolution concepts to solve complex problems in high-pressure team environments.</li>
      <li><strong>Constructive Feedback</strong>: Deliver actionable, constructive feedback within small group learning and managerial settings.</li>
      <li><strong>Initiative &amp; Ownership</strong>: Demonstrate personal initiative and proactive leadership in ambiguous environments.</li>
      <li><strong>Team Influence</strong>: Persuasively influence peers and team members without relying solely on formal authority.</li>
      <li><strong>Empowerment &amp; Leadership Analysis</strong>: Inspire and empower others by critically analyzing how leadership behaviors impact workforce performance.</li>
      <li><strong>Change Management</strong>: Demonstrate the diagnostic and strategic ability to steer organizational transformation and manage change.</li>
    </ol>
