## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Collaborative Capability**: Understand how to collaborate effectively with diverse parties across functional boundaries.
2. **Conflict Management**: Apply conflict resolution concepts to solve complex problems in high-pressure team environments.
3. **Constructive Feedback**: Deliver actionable, constructive feedback within small group learning and managerial settings.
4. **Initiative & Ownership**: Demonstrate personal initiative and proactive leadership in ambiguous environments.
5. **Team Influence**: Persuasively influence peers and team members without relying solely on formal authority.
6. **Empowerment & Leadership Analysis**: Inspire and empower others by critically analyzing how leadership behaviors and traits impact workforce performance and business results.
7. **Change Management**: Demonstrate the diagnostic and strategic ability to steer organizational transformation and manage change.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The curriculum moves in four progressive arcs: from the **individual** to the **team**, the **organization**, and ultimately **enterprise transformation**.

- **Weeks 1–4** establish self-awareness, personal values, personality differences, motivation, and influence.
- **Weeks 5–7** dissect team dynamics, interpersonal communication, corporate culture, and organizational politics.
- **Weeks 8–11** build human capital architecture, organization design, workforce planning, and talent management.
- **Weeks 12–14** focus on performance governance, compensation, transformational change, and employee engagement.
