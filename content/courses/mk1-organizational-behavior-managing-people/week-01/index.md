# Week 01: Strategizing Your Life: Self-Awareness, Values & Purpose

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Leadership effectiveness begins from within. In high-stakes organizational environments, executives who possess profound self-awareness, crystal-clear ethical anchors, and an explicit personal purpose demonstrate superior resilience, cognitive clarity, and decision-making consistency under pressure.

---

## 1. Core Frameworks & Theoretical Foundations

### The Self-Awareness Triad

- **Internal Self-Awareness**: Understanding one's own values, passions, aspirations, fit with environment, reactions (including thoughts, feelings, behaviors, strengths, and weaknesses), and impact on others.
- **External Self-Awareness**: Understanding how other people view us in terms of the same factors. Effective leaders actively solicit negative feedback to close the perception-reality gap.
- **Values Congruence**: The degree of alignment between personal core values and organizational behavior. Value misalignment is the primary driver of executive burnout and cynical disengagement.

### Strategizing Your Life (Christensen Framework)

Applying rigorous business strategy principles to personal leadership:

1. **Resource Allocation**: How you allocate your finite resources (time, energy, emotional bandwidth) is your actual strategy, regardless of stated intentions.
2. **The Motivation Trap**: Prioritizing hygiene factors (title, immediate compensation) over intrinsic motivators (mastery, autonomy, mission).
3. **Guardrails & Humility**: Maintaining absolute ethical boundaries ("100% of the time is easier than 98% of the time").

---

## 2. Managerial Implications for Indonesian Enterprises

- **Reflective Leadership**: Transitioning from reactive tactical management to strategic intent.
- **Authentic Executive Presence**: Building credibility through transparent, values-driven communication.
- **Navigating High-Power Distance**: Creating psychological safety for subordinates while maintaining executive decisiveness.
