# Week 02: Data-Driven Decision Making & Analytical Culture

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Investing millions in modern cloud data infrastructure yields zero ROI if the corporate culture remains tethered to HiPPO decision-making (_Highest Paid Person's Opinion_). Building a data-driven culture requires embedding statistical evidence into routine executive rituals.

---

## 1. Transforming from Intuition to Evidence

### The Davenport DELTA Framework (Competing on Analytics)

High-performing analytical organizations exhibit five foundational attributes:

- **D — Data**: High-quality, clean, unified, accessible, and governed enterprise data.
- **E — Enterprise**: Analytics managed as a centralized enterprise-wide capability rather than isolated departmental fiefdoms.
- **L — Leadership**: C-suite leaders who demand empirical evidence before approving capital allocation.
- **T — Targets**: Identifying the high-leverage strategic domains where analytics delivers game-changing competitive advantage (e.g., dynamic pricing for airlines, fraud detection for fintech).
- **A — Analysts**: Building multidisciplinary teams of data engineers, data scientists, and business translators.

### De-Biasing Data Interpretation

- **Correlation vs. Causation**: Spurious correlations and lurking confounders.
- **Survivorship Bias**: Analyzing only successful firms or surviving product lines while ignoring failed predecessors.
- **Goodhart's Law**: _"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure."_

---

## 2. Executive Takeaways

- Fostering an experimentation culture: Shifting from endless committee debates to rapid A/B testing and empirical iteration.
