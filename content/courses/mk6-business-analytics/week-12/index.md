# Week 12: Practical: Data Privacy, Cybersecurity & Compliance (UU PDP)

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Data is both an enterprise asset and a catastrophic legal and reputational liability. Executives must govern data collection under stringent privacy frameworks like Indonesia's **Undang-Undang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP No. 27/2022)** and establish proactive cybersecurity resilience.

---

## 1. Regulatory Governance: UU PDP & Global Benchmarks (GDPR)

### Core Principles of Data Protection

1. **Lawful Basis & Consent**: Explicit, recorded consent for specific processing purposes.
2. **Purpose Limitation**: Personal data collected for one stated purpose (e.g., shipping logistics) cannot be co-opted for another (e.g., third-party marketing).
3. **Data Minimization**: Collecting strictly the minimum data required to execute the transaction.
4. **Accuracy & Retention Limitation**: Deleting or anonymizing data once the legal purpose is fulfilled.
5. **Data Subject Rights**: Right to access, rectify, revoke consent, and demand deletion ("Right to be Forgotten").

### Corporate Obligations Under UU PDP

- Appointing a qualified **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** reporting to the board.
- Conducting mandatory **Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)** before launching high-risk analytics or automated profiling.
- Mandatory 72-hour notification to authorities and affected individuals in the event of a data breach.

---

## 2. Cybersecurity Governance for C-Suite Leaders

- The **CIA Triad**: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.
- Zero-Trust Architecture: Never trust, always verify; least-privilege access controls.
- Incident response playbooks and cyber insurance calibration.
