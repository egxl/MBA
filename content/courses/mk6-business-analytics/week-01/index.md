# Week 01: Introduction to Business Analytics

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Business Analytics is the scientific process of transforming raw organizational data into insights for making better, faster decisions. Analytics bridges the gap between technical data engineering and strategic executive leadership.

---

## 1. The Analytics Maturity Spectrum (Gartner Hierarchy)

```
                       ▲
                       │                                                    PRESCRIPTIVE
                       │                                                    "How can we make
                       │                               PREDICTIVE           it happen?"
                       │                               "What will happen?"  (Optimization,
                       │          DIAGNOSTIC           (Machine Learning,   Simulation)
                       │          "Why did it happen?" Forecasting)
        VALUE &        │ DESCRIPTIVE (Root Cause, Drilldown)
        COMPETITIVE    │ "What happened?"
        ADVANTAGE      │ (Reporting, BI, Dashboards)
                       └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────►
                                             DIFFICULTY & SOPHISTICATION
```

1. **Descriptive Analytics**: Summarizing historical data to understand past operational performance (KPI scorecards, variance analysis).
2. **Diagnostic Analytics**: Drilling down into correlations and anomalies to uncover why deviations occurred.
3. **Predictive Analytics**: Using statistical modeling and machine learning algorithms on historical patterns to forecast future outcomes.
4. **Prescriptive Analytics**: Using mathematical optimization, heuristics, and algorithmic rules to recommend the single best course of action under constraints.

---

## 2. Analytics Life Cycle in the Enterprise

- Problem Definition $\to$ Data Ingestion & Cleansing $\to$ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) $\to$ Model Building & Cross-Validation $\to$ Deployment $\to$ Continuous Monitoring.
