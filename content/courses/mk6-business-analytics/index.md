## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this course, participants will be able to:

1. **Core Problem Identification**: Identify and rigorously diagnose core operational and commercial dilemmas in business cases using empirical data.
2. **Contextual & Multi-Perspective Synthesis**: Build comprehensive analytical models integrating enterprise data, cross-functional constraints, and industry context.
3. **Data-Driven Solution Modeling**: Develop viable, evidence-backed strategic solution options based on predictive and prescriptive modeling.
4. **Executive Decision Execution**: Make responsible, mathematically sound decisions grounded in statistical models, big data ethics, and business intelligence.

---

## 🧭 Pedagogical Progression

The curriculum begins with analytics foundations, data-driven decision frameworks, and predictive modeling. **Weeks 4–8** apply these analytical techniques across five core functional domains: customer, marketing, finance, supply chains, and enterprise risk. **Weeks 9–12** place these applications within a governance framework covering big data architecture, ethical AI, enabling cloud technologies, and data privacy (UU PDP). Advanced business intelligence dashboards, executive visualization, and future analytics trends close the course in **Weeks 13–14**.
