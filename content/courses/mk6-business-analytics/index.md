## 🏛️ Syllabus Reference & Academic Governance

<div class="mk-governance-grid">

  <!-- Left Card: Learning Outcomes -->
  <div class="mk-gov-card">
    <h3>🎯 Course Learning Outcomes</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Core Problem Identification</strong>: Identify and rigorously diagnose core operational and commercial dilemmas in business cases using empirical data.</li>
      <li><strong>Contextual &amp; Multi-Perspective Synthesis</strong>: Build comprehensive analytical models integrating enterprise data, cross-functional constraints, and industry context.</li>
      <li><strong>Data-Driven Solution Modeling</strong>: Develop viable, evidence-backed strategic solution options based on predictive and prescriptive modeling.</li>
      <li><strong>Executive Decision Execution</strong>: Make responsible, mathematically sound decisions grounded in statistical models, big data ethics, and business intelligence.</li>
    </ol>
